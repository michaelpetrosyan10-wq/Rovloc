Every voice here is a computer voice. No real person's voice was copied, and nobody was recorded for this page.

Made on our computer with Chatterbox and Chatterbox Turbo by Resemble AI (MIT licence). Their voices were designed by us from the built-in voices of Kokoro 82M by hexgrad (Apache-2.0; its training data includes Koniwa, CC BY 3.0, and SIWIS, CC BY 4.0). Pronunciation for Kokoro by eSpeak NG (GPL-3.0), used only on our computer; none of it is in these files.

The H voices were made with Higgsfield's stock voices (text to speech, ElevenLabs engine) on the game owner's account.

The monster versions are our own processing of Danny's lines: played slower (plain resampling, like a slowed tape), a whisper made from the line itself, a quiet copy an octave lower, a synthetic breath and synthetic rooms. Monster 9's deep voice is a mix of two of Kokoro's built-in voices, spoken by Chatterbox. Checked with Whisper (MIT) through faster-whisper (MIT).
