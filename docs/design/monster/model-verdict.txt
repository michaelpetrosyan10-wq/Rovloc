DEFECTS, WORST FIRST (each with what to do)
  1. The giraffe/heron neck. The single biggest "bird" signal: a thin bent stalk with a small head on top
     (turntable, lean, catch). The favourite shows NO neck; the head comes out from under the hood/hair.
     -> Cut the neck to a short thick human neck, mostly hidden: put the torn hood UP over the back of the
     skull (or the hair long and dark around it) so the face hangs out of a dark hole. The face's height comes
     from the face, not from the neck.
  2. Face reads as a mask/beak, not flesh. Hard black rim line round the face outline, the lower half a smooth
     straight cone, cheeks flat. -> Kill the black rim; let the face blend into hair and skull with skin
     continuing round the sides. Give the lower face the favourite's soft swell: the hollows beside the nose
     ridge, the slightly bulging middle, a visible slit mouth with lips near the tip, the seam line. Make the
     point soft and fleshy, not a beak tip.
  3. Brow wrinkles are even printed stripes (like a corrugated shell / tiger stripes). -> Fewer, irregular,
     deeper furrows that fade at the sides, plus the vertical crease between the eye holes as in the picture;
     shade them with the normal map, not dark painted lines.
  4. The arms and legs are smooth tubes (pool noodles), huge in frame, lit flat white. The hands in close-ups
     look like rubber gloves. -> Bony knobbly forearms with tendons and the dark veined, leathery back of hand
     from the favourite (darker, more brown-grey than the face); darken limbs so they stay in shadow; show the
     hand gripping something (locker edge / door frame) near the camera -- that hand is half the fear in the picture.
  5. Clothes look like a game NPC: blue jeans + grey crop top + bare ribcage = zombie-from-a-store-asset. The
     favourite has a big dark heavy hoodie and almost no body. -> Make the hoodie long and dark, swallowing the
     torso; jeans near-black; no bright blue anywhere; cover the pale bare belly/ribs that show as a white mass
     behind the head in (b).
  6. Too much light on him. He is lit evenly grey-white in the corridor; the favourite is pitch black with the
     face as the only pale thing. -> Body and limbs fall to black; only the face (and the hand) catch the torch.
  7. Eye holes: good, keep. Slightly too round/bulgy in the catch, and the wet rim highlight looks like goggles.
     -> More slanted almond, flatter, no rim glint; pinprick tiny and white.
  8. Streaks: right idea, but in Max they branch like cracks and in Potato they multiply into 3-4 stripes.
     -> Exactly two thin dark dry streaks straight down from the inner bottom of each eye hole.
