## 3. Staging rules for the whole scene

1. **The camera is the party.** Handheld, eye height. Inserts (marked INSERT) show what nobody is pointing at and
   contain no face.
2. **One readable face: Danny's.** Four lip-synced shots (SYNC), each one line of 3 s or less, Danny standing or
   crouched and still, three-quarter to the lens, nothing else changing in the frame. Every other line is OFF: over a
   back, a profile, an insert, or from behind the camera.
3. **A state changes only at a cut.** Lid open or shut, the thing there or gone, its face upside down: each is set by
   a clip's start frame. Nothing timed happens in the background of a clip. One action per clip.
4. **Light.** Overcast afternoon, grey and flat. Danny's face is in soft shade. At 38.6 the cloud thickens and the yard
   lamp buzzes on by itself; from then the lamp, above and behind him, is the key light and the sky stays dark grey. It
   never turns to night before the time-lapse (R3): it is a quarter past three. The engine's day value (0.65, then 0.5
   from 39.6) is the reference; a video, if made, is graded to it. The grade, the lamp coming on, the lamp's dips and
   the whip blur are made in the edit, not generated.
5. **The video file carries no audio.** Every line, laugh, chirp, thud and slam is a timeline event of the game, so
   subtitles, "names Off", the twins, the Short cut, the mix and the Roblox port all keep working and a muted video may
   autoplay.
6. **No readable text in any clip.** Cards are drawn by the website. The quiz paper shows its blank side.
7. **Three laughs, no more:** the group after "I studied for that!", Marcus after "not yet, sir", Danny after the hat
   line. Each under 1.2 s, layered, under a line, never a solo laugh on camera. Everything else is breath.
8. **Voices first.** The picture is cut to the locked audio. The player hears the jokes on a sound board before any
   video credit is spent.

## 4. The thing: really cute first, wrong the second he looks away

**Cute (18.9 to 30.1, while Danny looks at it).** One simple fluff ball, the size of a kitten curled up, covered in
pale down like a duckling. A porcelain doll's face no wider than a jam-jar lid, round and sweet: it is what will
grow into the Tall Man's long face, but nothing about it says so yet. Glossy and **uncracked**, two big dark oval eyes, a tiny painted smile whose right corner curls up. It moves only
by hopping, like a small frog: the fluff squashes flat, it flicks up, it lands with a soft pat. Its sound is a
two-note chirp, low then high, like a question (built on the game's own music-box tick). It bumps into his shoe and
rolls onto its back. It tilts its face up at him like a puppy.

**Wrong (from 30.1, the cut after Danny turns to tell you it is cute).**
1. The chirp stops in the middle of a note. It was for him.
2. The tilt does not stop. The face keeps turning, smoothly, on a body that does not move, until it is upside down.
   The smile is a frown. The two notes come back the wrong way round, high then low, at half speed.
3. The round body opens like a hand. What looked like a soft body was eight long pale fingers folded over one another
   like a fist; the down is only fuzz on the knuckles. It stands on them, twice as tall as it sat.
4. It does not hop again. It walks, quick and soft, up his sleeve.
5. On his shoulder it folds round again, right way up, smiling. When he glances at it, it is cute.

It never bites, grips or hurts him in anything we see. It sits on him like a pet. He likes it. He is not attacked, he
is chosen.
