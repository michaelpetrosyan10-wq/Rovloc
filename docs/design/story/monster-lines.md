Story editor's final, 16 Nov 2026. Host Danny. Companion to opening-v3.md (line numbers L1 to L23 are that file's).
Everything here is original. No blood, no gore, nothing shown or said about harm to anyone he catches (I12).
Second pass, 17 Nov: five line fixes (18, 21, 43, N13), the name rule for players called Danny, Kayla or Marcus,
the laugh timing, and the monster's pace written as 0.82.

- 99 and 82 lines became **63** (47 without a name, 16 with one), plus 16 twins: 79 strings to render before the name
  bank. The player asked for about 50 and "dont overdo it"; the brief asks for at least 60.

pre-rendered name can be joined at a pause.

## 2. Rules of use

1. **Rare.** Two budgets.
   - *Unprompted* lines (G1, G2, G3, and G9 and G10 when they ride on those): at most **one per 120 s per listener**
     and **one per 45 s team-wide** (monster.md 3.2, H5).
   - *Prompted* lines (G4 to G8: something just happened) play by chance per event (section 5).
   - Over a reference trace the mean is **one line per 60 to 100 s per listener** outside chases.
2. **Never two close together.** At least **12 s** between any two lines one listener hears (3 s for Ready, Chase and
   Stunned); at most **3 per rolling 60 s**; an unprompted line needs 20 s with no speech before it; a chase carries
   at most 2 lines.
3. **A line is the interaction.** It replaces the tell it would have come with. It is never stacked on a stinger, and
   none plays within 1.5 s of a stinger, a reveal or a catch (G8 waits 2.5 s after the catch).
4. **Never explains.** No line may contain: mask, bin, dumpster, crate, host, parasite, Walter, Michael, 1989, gun,
   bullet, tower, or a hint, a direction or how to beat him. "It" is allowed and is never named. A word list in
   `VoiceConfig`'s validator holds this.
5. **Honest (I1).** He is really where the voice is, and the group matches where he is: a G3 line only from a real
   duct or grate, a G2 line only through exactly one wall. Through two walls or more he uses no words (breath, nails):
   under 1,100 Hz speech cannot be understood. "Is something funny?" (11, 47) only after a real noise by that
   listener. "I was looking right at you" and the G5 lines only with a real line of sight.
6. **The big memories are spent once.** Lines 1, 6, 11, 12, 22 and 47, the five with "so cute" (13, 17, 24, 37, 44)
   and N4, N8, N14: each at most **once per stage per listener**; no two of them within **5 minutes** for one
   listener; none in a stage's first 2 minutes; "so cute" at most **3 times a run** per listener. Line 8 ("It doesn't
   come off. I tried.") once a run, Auditorium or later.
7. **Earned.** The opening has already given him a voice, so the first line in play may be line 1. No line in a
   stage's first 25 s.
8. **Understood.** Every finished line, after the monster processing and after the one-wall filter where it applies,
   must come back word for word from an automatic transcription (10 % word errors at most; none on a name), or its
   processing is backed off. Subtitles always: struck-through DANNY, the direction glyph, italics for the ear.
9. **The voice never changes the brain.** It is not a noise the AI hears.

## 3. The name rule

- Every line with `{name}` has a twin that needs none and is a complete line by itself.
- The name is spoken only by the monster, from a **bank of pre-rendered first names** in his processed voice, in
  three shapes: first word before a pause ("{name}? …", "{name}. …", "{name}, …"), last word after a comma
  ("…, {name}." and "…, {name}?"), and standing alone ("{name}."). The whisper lines need the same three shapes in the
  whisper voice.
- The twin plays when the name is not in the bank, is filtered by `NameSanitizer`, is empty, or "The monster says
  names" is Off. The subtitle shows what was actually spoken.
- How often: the stage's probability applies to prompted lines (0.3 Basement, 0.5 Hallways, 0.7 Auditorium, 0.9 roof
  and city) and is halved for unprompted lines (0.15, 0.25, 0.35, 0.45). The same name is not said to one listener
  twice within 90 s.
- Clean Danny never says a player's name, in the opening or anywhere before the standoff's authored lines.
- A player whose name is Danny, Kayla or Marcus always gets the twin (a player called Danny would otherwise hear
  N1 as "Danny? It's me. It's Danny.").

## 4. Voice direction: Danny's voice gone wrong

- **It is Danny.** The same voice as the boy who said "This is so cute", rendered clean first and then processed
  (sound.md 2.5): calm and slightly too slow (rate 0.82, the figure the slot checks use); an audible breath in before
  the line and none after; the main layer 3 to 4 semitones down with the formants 8 to 12 % down and the 1 to 4 kHz band left alone; a whisper double of
  the same words 10 to 14 dB under and 25 to 40 ms late; a sub layer felt, not heard; a faint ring as if from inside
  porcelain.
- **He almost never raises his voice.** G7 is the only group above speaking level, and it is a boy calling after his
  friends, not a roar. He never laughs. He never sounds angry. Fond is worse.
- **Per group.** G1: the clearest, the least processed, a lost kid. G2: lower, slower, half to himself. G3: close and
  pleased, the kitten voice from "Hey, little guy." G4: a dry whisper, 2 to 4 dB under the Head lines. G5: curious.
  G6: mild, a little disappointed. G8: gentle. G9: kind, which is the horror of it. G10: amused.
- **"Is something funny?"** is always built from the teacher-voice take of L4 (low, slow, flat), never rendered fresh,
  so the audience knows the voice at once.
- **The arc over the night.** Basement: the lost kid (G1, G9). Hallways: the thing (G2, G5). Auditorium: vents and
  hides (G3, G4). The roof: the blame lines and the pity line (N4, N8, N14, N15, line 8), one in five rendered half
  clean, which sets up the standoff's "It's me. It's still me."
- **On Gus (the city).** The janitor's own lines, unchanged. Because it keeps every host's memories, Gus's voice says
  exactly one of Danny's lines, once a run, on the Rooftops: "Wait up, you guys."
