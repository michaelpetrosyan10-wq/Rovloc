    floating eyes. The reference's life is many tiny things moving together, lit by where they are.
    Fix: mote brightness = the marched in-scatter at its own position (invisible in the dark, glittering only inside
    the torch cone, shafts and lamp glow); a size/brightness spread with most of them tiny and faint; short motion
    streaks; cluster them by drafts (curl noise with a flow direction per room, eddies at doors). Moths as small dark
    flutter silhouettes that catch light, swarming the lamp. Add life that is not dust: water dripping from pipes,
    swaying cables and pendants (they exist, make them visibly move in stills: frame-to-frame blur), paper sliding in

    Fix: start the torch's air march a short distance past the lens, soften the cone edge (penumbra + cookie with

    Fix: blur the reflection by roughness and contact distance and break it with rain-ring normals; irregular puddle
