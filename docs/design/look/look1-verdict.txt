DEFECTS, WORST FIRST, WITH THE FIX
 1. The first-person hands and torch are blocky, flat-coloured, lit the same in every room (bright yellow-tan
    in the green lockers room, glowing in the red basement). They sit in every shot, bottom right, and shout
    "Roblox". Fix: a real hand/sleeve/torch model with skin and fabric shading, lit by the scene (ambient from the
    haze colour, torch bounce), darker in dark rooms, with a little sway and breathing. Highest value per hour.
 2. Too little moving life. The reference has hundreds to thousands of moving specks per frame; these have
    roughly 5-20 dust motes per view and the moths are not visible in any still. Fix: GPU-instanced dust (1-4k on
    Max, a few hundred on Medium) drifting on curl noise, lit by the volume march so it only sparkles inside beams;
    visible moth swarms at every burning lamp; drifting ash/paper on the floor; steam lit by the air.
 3. Backlot reflection is a sharp mirror copy of the dumpster with comb/scanline stair-steps, sitting on asphalt
    that is otherwise pitch black and not visibly wet. Looks like a bug, not rain. Fix: blur SSR by roughness and
    distance from contact, break it with the puddle mask, ripple it with the rain-ring normals, fade at SSR edges,
    add TAA; give the whole ground a wet sheen; add splashes, a lit yard lamp in frame, and real cloud/lightning sky.
    Also replace the flat blue city blocks and fix the dumpster's low-res pixelated texture (mip/filter or higher res).
 4. Lockers and failing barely changed. Lockers: the torch hotspot is a flat yellow disc on the wall, the locker is
    a clean sharp box with no worn edges, and the room is evenly lit green: too bright to be scary. Failing: some
    dust and gloss only; the dying bulb does not throw moving shadows. Fix: torch cookie (textured beam with rings
    and a dark centre core falloff), edge wear on lockers/doors/frames, crush the ambient in lockers so only the
    torch reads, dying-bulb shadow + flicker relighting the room.
 5. The window shafts read as one glowing slab with a hard flat top edge at the ceiling, not separate rays from each
    board gap. Fix: finer march (or quarter-res, more steps, depth-aware upsample), fade the shaft at its source
    and top, strengthen the gaps between rays; put moonlight shafts somewhere besides the 4 Main Office windows
    (skylights, door gaps, vents) so the wow shot is not one room.
 6. Surfaces still look plastic/flat at a distance: walls are one texture stretched over big boxes, no grout depth,
    no damage geometry (cables, hanging tiles, broken panels). Fix: per-material detail table (grout, flakes,
    stains), parallax/bump on tiles, a few cheap clutter props per corridor (hanging cables, a swinging lamp that
    carries its light, papers that move in a draught).
 7. Dark areas with nothing in them: left/right black walls in failing and monster, black floor in windows. Not
    scary-dark, just empty. Fix: low faint bounce or a far dim light so shapes read; keep pure black only for
    deliberate hiding spots.
 8. Monster view unchanged in character (still the same thin silhouette in haze). It works, but the "after" adds
    almost nothing. Fix: let the shaft/lamp air pass in front of and behind him so he cuts the haze; faint floor
    reflection stronger on wet tiles.
