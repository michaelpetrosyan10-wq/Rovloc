WHAT WAS BUILT, AND HOW
Tool: web/tools/look-shots.mjs (+ look-shots-entry.js). Six fixed views (floor, root, direction, torch, clock moment,
the Creature placed by the scripted handle), shot at any tier; --visibility measures how much the Creature changes its
screen bounds; --perf is the gate's frame-time method (check.mjs section 3: sandbox site, 320x180, CPU 4x throttle,
the gate's four places, torch on, 60 frames, median + p95); --gate prints the look gate's numbers; --site serves an
older build for A/B. Views: lockers = a locker in its alcove (the school has no locker rows: one locker per alcove);
windows = the Main Office's boarded windows (the only 4 windows in the school, all on L1).

1 Camera (renderer.js, budget.film: Max, High, Medium)
  - Half-float frame. Materials still write display values (so the Creature's and every other shader stays valid), but
    the world material adds what lies far past white (exposed linear > 1.8) above 1.0: tubes, bulbs, the torch's
    hotspot, their reflections. Bloom reads that headroom, so truly bright things bloom hardest.
  - Film shoulder in the post: above 0.8 the brightest channel rolls off with its hue held; headroom bleaches to white
    (a bulb's white core inside a coloured glow). Below 0.8 nothing changes: darks and mids stay exactly as on Potato.
  - Eyes: a 1x1 ping-pong pass keeps the frame's log key and a slowly following "used to" key (up 1.6/s, down 0.45/s);
    exposure = (now/used)^0.6, clamped 0.4..2.4. At rest it is exactly 1 (tier parity); a change of light dazzles or
    blinds for a moment. Measured (monster view, Max): torch off 22.9 -> 12.3 -> 10.7 at 0.3 s (blind) -> 12.3 at 8 s;
    torch on -> 25.0 at 0.3 s (glare) -> 21.4 at 3 s.
  - Colour script per floor (atmosphere.js LOOK_SCRIPT): split-toning shadows cold / lights warm with unit-luminance
    tints (hue only): Basement sodium vs cold blue, Hallways sick green vs moonlight, Auditorium emergency red vs deep
    blue, outdoors sodium vs storm blue. Finer two-layer grain on the clock, heavier in shadows. Lens fringe and
    vignette were already there.
  Low / Potato: none of it is compiled (post shader identical); they keep their 8-bit frame.
2 Air (renderer.js VOL pass; Max 28 steps / 70 studs, High 14 / 55, Medium 8 / 40; half resolution)
  - One march per pixel: the torch's two cones (cut by its shadow map), the nearest burning fixtures (Max 4, High 3,
    Medium 2, at their live flicker), and on Max/High the windows' moonlight: each window near the camera renders an
    orthographic depth tile along the moon's direction (boards, frames, the Creature cut the shafts), with swaying
    branch shadows from noise. Density drifts (two octaves of 3D value noise on the clock) plus the level's floor mist
    (Basement). Henyey-Greenstein forward scattering (g 0.5): beams glow seen from the side or looking into them and
    lay almost no veil when looking down your own torch (this is what keeps the monster readable). Jitter changes per
    frame and blends 55 % with the last frame; a tent filter upsamples.
  - Haze colour from the nearest lights (atmosphere.js, every tier, a uniform: free on Potato): the fog keeps the
    film's luminance and leans toward the hue of the fixtures within 38 studs (scan at 3 Hz, camera level only).
  - ONE haze fall-off for every tier (lighting.js HAZE_CLOSE 160 studs, exp^2, darkened as before). Before, the haze
    closed at the tier's view distance (Potato 120, Max infinite): Potato players saw the monster less. Now identical;
    each tier's draw distance (Potato 192) ends where the haze is already 98.7 % closed.
  - The torch's old additive haze ribbons are gone (Medium now marches instead; the switch would have been dead).
  - Dust: the motes are lit by the marched fixtures too and show twice as bright on the film tiers; the shaft dust
    seeds are now seeded, not Math.random (pictures repeat exactly).
3 Surfaces (materials.js LOOK = budget.detail: Max 3, High 2, Medium 1; Low/Potato compile none)

  - Wet floors per level (Basement 0.62, outdoors 0.9, Hallways 0.28...): darker, glossier, puddles where noise pools,
    mirror-flat, with expanding rings (rain outdoors, drips in the Basement). Mirror share goes out in the alpha.
  - Screen-space reflections (Max full res 30 steps, High half res 20; Medium none): wet floors mirror the corridor,
    lamps, the dumpster, the Creature.

ROBLOX
The port can map nearly every idea to built-in features. The film camera is Lighting.Technology = Future with a
script easing ExposureCompensation for the eyes (rest value from LightingMood, transient only), a BloomEffect whose
Threshold sits near white so only Neon, light parts and the torch bloom, and a ColorCorrectionEffect per floor (the
colour script's average tint; Roblox has no split-toning, so warm and cold come from the lights themselves). The air
is Atmosphere (Density, Haze, and Color/Decay set per zone from the nearest lights' hue, the same fall-off on every
quality level, as here), plus Beam shafts under fixtures and window Beams aimed along the moon direction with a
scrolling branch texture (ParticleEmitters with LightInfluence for the dust and the moths, which then light from the
real lamps). Surfaces are SurfaceAppearance / MaterialVariant PBR sets (normal, roughness, metalness) with
Reflectance on wet floors; Future lighting gives real specular, and Roblox's own screen-space reflections are not
exposed, so puddles would be glossy material, not mirrors. Shadows are free in Future (SpotLight torch and every
fixture with Shadows = true), which covers the dying bulb's moving shadows the web lacks. Small life is TweenService
on hanging lamps with their PointLight parented (they carry their light), ParticleEmitters for rain (attached to a
part following the camera), splashes and steam, and Lighting flashes for lightning; rain rings need a flipbook
texture, which is an image file and so outside this pass's rules.

WHAT WAS ADDED
1 Photographed surfaces (9cf429b, e09246a; tools/scans.py + scans.json, `npm run scans`). Nine CC0 Poly Haven scans
  stand behind palette keys on the film tiers: Max 4096, High 2048, Medium 1024 px (colour WebP q92, OpenGL normal and
  AO/rough/height packed as 4:4:4 JPEG q95). Real-world sizes (1 stud = 0.28 m: tiles 1.5 m per repeat, brick and
  plaster 3 m, damaged concrete 5 m). Organic surfaces are hex-tiled with random offsets (and rotations where they
  allow it), so no repeat lines up; tiles, planks and brick are sampled once on a grid with parallax occlusion from
  the height map (Max 48 steps, High/Medium 16). Each scan's colour is scaled so the surface keeps exactly the mean
  albedo the generated texture (or the bare part colour) gave it: the photograph adds stains, chips and grain, never
  brightness, so the place and the Creature in it read as bright as on Low and Potato. Our own noise (LOOK detail)
  still runs on top. Low and Potato keep the generated 512 and 256 sets byte for byte.
2 Edges and wear (d541a70). A screen-space pass on Max ('Full', 12 taps) and High ('Lite', 8): from the depth buffer
  every pixel compares a ring of neighbours a bevel's width (0.06 studs on the surface) away with its own plane, giving
  a bevel highlight on every convex edge, paint worn through along it (broken by world-space noise: chips and runs),
  and grime in every inside corner (floor meets wall, frames, skirtings); silhouettes never glow. In the materials:
  dents and scratches in metal (lockers, doors, pipes), leak rings on ceilings, rust runs and a damp ring under the
  nearest dripping or steaming pipes (atmosphere._scanStains, film tiers only).
3 Hands and torch (a51fb7c, 84c347d; tools/art/hands). hand.py drives Blender (bpy) with the MakeHuman CC0 base mesh
  through MPFB2 (pinned commit, hashed files: tools/art/hands/sources.json): the right hand's four fingers closed in a
  power grip fitted by least squares round a 5 cm D-cell barrel, the thumb searched onto the barrel, the wrist bent,
  cut at the forearm, subdivided twice, vertex-coloured (skin variation, nails, ray-traced occlusion against hand and
  barrel). web/art/hands/hand-right.bin.gz: 26,569 vertices, 53,056 triangles, 734 KB, fetched by Max and High only
  (manifest.hands; budget.handModel). The torch is shaped to the grip (knurled barrel, bezel, lens), lit by the scene
  like the world plus the torch's own spill on the fingers, with the existing breathing sway and turn lag. Medium,
  Low and Potato keep the hand shaped in code and never fetch the model.
4 Sharp pictures (7bf5780). Temporal anti-aliasing on Max and High: Halton(2,3) sub-pixel jitter over 16 frames, the
  history reprojected through last frame's camera and read with a 5-tap Catmull-Rom, held to the current pixel's
  YCoCg variance box, Karis-weighted; the hands drawn after it unjittered. Max+ (the Supersample setting) is now 200 %.
  The picture tool draws 12 frames per still on TAA tiers; --supersample shoots Max+ (24 min per 1080p view in
  software, so the finals are Max with TAA, not Max+).
5 Download (e06de9e, after the finals). The film tiers' set listings (1024/2048/4096: maps, scans, keys) moved out of
  the page into assets/textures-<size>.json, fetched only with that set; the engine's GLSL is compacted at bundle time
  (tools/glsl-compact.mjs: comments, indentation, spare spaces; every build checks each compacted shader token for
  token against the original and stops on any difference). Verified: windows + lockers at all five tiers from an
  uncompacted --no-minify build and the compacted build are byte-identical PNGs (look1/eq). The gate (check.mjs) now
  counts those listings' maps and manifest.hands among the site's assets (the hand asset would have shown as unused).
