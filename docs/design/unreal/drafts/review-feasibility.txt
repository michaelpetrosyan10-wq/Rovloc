STAY BEHIND - FEASIBILITY REVIEW (can this be built, and what is it hiding)
Reviewer lens: one Windows PC, UE 5.8.4, an AI builder working by command line and scripts, free/CC0 assets,
MetaHuman + Megascans, no mocap, no artists, usage limits. Fifteen findings, worst first.

VERDICT: The production draft is careful about process, tools and licences, but it sizes the game as if the
hard part were code. The hidden costs are art and animation: seven more creature bodies with no source, an
alien ship with no asset source, a city seen from the air, too many people, and a hunter whose movement nobody
here can hand-animate. It is also written for a PC it calls "unknown", but that PC has already been read
(RTX 5070 12 GB, 16 GB RAM, 611 GB). Fix 1-6 before the owner approves. The first slice is mostly the right
one, but it must also measure what one extra creature costs.

RELATIVE BUILD EFFORT (reviewer's estimate, Chapter 2 = 1.0):
Prologue 1.0 | Ch1 basement 1.8 (all core systems, the 8-person room) | Ch2 1.0 | Ch3 1.2 (theatre, fly tower)
| Ch4 1.0 | Ch5 1.5 (Swarm, roof, helicopter, fall) | Ch6 city 2.5 | Ch7 flight 1.2 | Ch8 mothership 3.5
| Epilogue 0.5. The draft's schedule rates the city at about 1.5x a floor and the mothership at about 2x.

1. [BLOCKER] 6-production s2 (Step 0 table), s4 (quality ladder), s10 (drivers 2) - The plan says "Unknown: the graphics
card, the memory and the disk". requirements.md s9 says the machine has been read: an RTX 5070 with 12 GB, 16 GB of RAM,
611 GB free. By the draft's own table, that means "High at 1440p with upscaling" and "may run out of memory", and the
disk is below its own 1 TB "comfortable" figure, while the plan needs about 500 GB. Epic's City Sample docs list 64 GB
of RAM (checked), and a 5.8 forum report shows MetaHuman skin-cache VRAM overflowing on 8 GB with only a few NPCs.
FIX: Rewrite s2, s4 and s10 for this machine:
- the look is judged on High at 1080p native or 1440p with DLSS; Ultra is for pictures only;
- the RAM upgrade (32 GB, 64 GB for the city) becomes decision 0, taken before the slice;
- the second drive becomes required (keep at most two packaged builds, and put the DDC and backups on it);
- City Sample is never opened whole: the spike extracts only the buildings it needs into a small new level and
  records the editor's peak memory on 16 GB;
- if the RAM stays at 16 GB, the high end of every range becomes the base estimate.

2. [BLOCKER] 2-creatures s2, s3 and s5; 6-production s3 and s9 - Seven creature kinds, five more "natural forms" on the
mothership, and eight different graphic mauls (fingers over the face, the fan closing over the head, the front opening,
and so on). Only the hunter has a model pipeline. 6-production s5 says Higgsfield is weak for 3D creatures, and the
plan has no line for creature art or animation anywhere. FIX: Add a creature production table to 6-production, giving
each kind a mesh source, a rig and a cost in weeks:
- Listeners, Mimics, Patrol and Brute: UE mannequin family, with walking retargeted from the Game Animation Sample;
- Still Ones: about 25 static poses and no walking;
- Dark Dwellers: a procedural Control Rig sheet;
- Swarm: Niagara with a few lead actors, and no Mass.
The mothership forms become material and scale variants of the same meshes, with no new rigs. Every maul becomes one
first-person template: a 1 to 2 s creature-specific close camera move, contact, blood on the screen and decals, cut to
black, and the sound carries the gore. The owner signs off each creature's look as a gate.

3. [BLOCKER] 6-production s4 ("Where realism will be strongest") and s10 M8; 4-puzzles-world s6 - Every realism promise
rests on Megascans and City Sample, which are Earth surfaces. The two-hour mothership has none of its own: hangar,
underdeck, garden deck, three holds, nest, Spine, core, pod bay, trophy hall. It is the largest chapter, rated 10 to 18
weeks against 8 to 14 for the city. FIX:
- Choose an alien art language that can be built from what exists: an organic ship made from Megascans organic scans
  (bone, rock, wet membrane), Geometry Script/PCG modules and emissive glyph decals.
- Build one modular hall/corridor kit and reuse it, with four distinct deck looks: Hangar; Underdeck+Garden; Holds+Nest;
  Spine+Core.
- Run a two-week alien-kit spike alongside M1, with a photo spot the owner judges.
- Re-size M8 to 14 to 24 weeks.

4. [MAJOR] 3-systems s13 (segments: off the street, tower canyon, under the bridge, the city burning below); 1-story
Ch7.1 ("lift-off between towers"); 6-production s10 M7 (3-6 wks) - This is a city seen from the air at flying speed:
World Partition streaming far beyond "a few dense blocks", on 16 GB. FIX: The ship lifts off from the Ch6 landing pad
and climbs straight above the roofline. The city below is a far field: City Sample HLOD proxies or a backdrop card with
light points, under cloud and darkness. The segments become rooftops (the Ch6 block), the cloud layer, the alien craft
pack, the outer fins and the hangar mouth. Cut the canyon and the bridge. Keep the owner's 15 to 20 minutes through the
course's length and its encounters, not through new environments.

5. [MAJOR] 6-production s9 and s10 - Chapters go one after another all the way to Final, so the city, flight and
mothership are first met 12 to 20 months in. M2 to M4 are sized the same (the theatre floor is not). Nothing states how
many builder hours a week the estimate assumes, or how much the usage limits will cost. FIX: After the slice and M1,
add M1.5, "the whole game in grey": every chapter blocked out, every creature as a grey stand-in with its real rule,
every puzzle working, cutscenes as animatics. The owner plays all nine hours grey, then the cuts are made. Re-state the
schedule in measured builder-days per effort unit (table above), with an explicit 25 to 35% loss to usage limits.

6. [MAJOR] 6-production s6 ("add its own crawl, fold and stoop by hand") and s3 ("three heights"); 2-creatures s1.2 and
s1.9 - The hunter must fold a 3 m body through 70x70 cm, hang head-down, climb lockers and rails, pass doors hand first,
lift ceiling tiles with its fingers, gallop over city roofs at 6 m/s and haul itself through low gravity. An AI agent
cannot hand-key that to the owner's "scary before odd". A navmesh agent also has one height, so "three navigation
heights" is not native. FIX: Give each seen move a method:
- stoop and upright walk: retargeted motion matching plus spine and neck offsets in Control Rig, driven by a ceiling
  trace;
- crawl: a procedural leg solver;
- the fold: never seen in play (it enters ducts out of sight, heard only), with one authored cutscene shot;
- the locker lean (the favourite picture's pose): a few authored poses blended in place;
- the city: rooftop silhouettes on splines instead of a gallop.
Use one navmesh at crawl height, with nav areas tagged by ceiling height choosing the pose, plus the authored duct graph.
Gate in the slice: three 10 s clips (corridor stoop, door pass, locker lean) that the owner judges.

7. [MAJOR] All drafts - Contradictions the builder would have to encode in Data/:
- three different noise tables (hunter threshold 25 / 28 / 30 dB; walk 45 / 42; a closed door 15 / 12 and a wall 30 / 20),
  and each calls itself "the one table";
- Danny's speeds: crawl 1.2 m/s (creatures) against 0.5 m/s (systems). With 0.5, the hunter's 1.1 m/s duct crawl
  outpaces him and breaks the "always stay ahead in a duct" rule;
- Marcus: one of the six survivors (systems) or taken at halftime (story);
- the breach: after Ch3 through the boiler wall (systems), Ch5 by the Mark (story), Ch5 by a drained generator (creatures);
- the event: a dance (balloons, glitter, glow sticks) or a basketball game;
- phones: story kills every lithium battery, which also kills the camcorder, flash camera and thermal camera, while
  audio has phones that buzz and Song 1 playing from one;
- Listeners click (audio) or do not (creatures);
- low gravity: 10 or 20 minutes;
- the library on F2 or F3;
- on the Mimic floor, Kayla on the radio (audio D4) or in person (story);
- the hunter's first whisper voices.
FIX: Before the map goes to the owner, write one canon sheet with a single owner for each number and fact: audio owns
the noise table, creatures own the thresholds, systems own Danny's speeds (with crawl at 1.2 m/s or faster), story owns
cast and timing. Every draft cites the sheet and keeps no copy of its own.

8. [MAJOR] 1-story s7 against 6-production s4 ("no crowds moving, no fights, no hugs, no handing things over") - Four
cutscenes break that rule:
- 8, boarding: a hovering helicopter, wind, crew, survivors climbing in, Gus stepping back;
- 9: Kayla clips the chute onto Danny and shoves him out of a spinning cabin;
- 3: the hunter's crawl and unfold, hand-keyed;
- 4: all eight people for 120 s.
FIX:
- 8: the helicopter is set down with its rotors turning; faces in close-up from inside the cabin, the boarders off frame,
  and Gus stepping back seen through the door;
- 9: played in first person (her hands on the harness, her face, the shove as the camera is thrown), which is the draft's
  own rule for "moments that would expose us";
- 3: in rain and floodlight, hands and silhouette, the unfold covered by a cut;
- 4: a seated circle cut by face, one speaker at a time.
Then re-rate the difficulty of every scene.

9. [MAJOR] 6-production s5 ("the taken in the holding halls (5.8 Collections)"); 1-story s3 and Ch8; 4-puzzles s8.3 - The
cast has 15 to 18 people with faces: the eight in the shelter, Marcus, Caleb, Owen's sister, the laundromat boy, the
library student, the IT technician, the crew and the crowd at the gate. On top of that come rows of sleepers. MetaHuman
Collections is Experimental in 5.8 (checked), and the VRAM report in finding 1 applies. FIX: Cap the cast at 10 speaking
MetaHumans with faces (Danny, Kayla, the six, Caleb, Marcus). Everyone else is heard and not seen, seen from behind or far
off, or under frosted lids (two posed static bodies, instanced with material variation). The rescue crew share one
helmeted body. Collections are used only after a spike passes on the 5070.

10. [MAJOR] 6-production s10, cut order and "Never cut"
Three steps cut things the owner set himself:
- step 7 merges Ch3 and Ch4, against his four floors with four monsters;
- step 3 (flight to 10 min) and step 6 (mothership to 90 min) go below his own 15-20 min and 2 h.
The "Never cut" list also leaves out most of what he asked for by name.
What should go first is not owner-named:
- the mothership "natural forms";
- the seven-habit learning ledger;
- the NG+ endings, including the drive-thru;
- Easter egg 7, a playable copy of the scrapped web game, which needs a browser runtime inside UE;
- photo mode;
- the Low and Medium quality tiers;
- the dumbwaiter;
- extra side places.
FIX: Re-order s10 with the items above first, and steps 3 and 6 last. Delete step 7. Write a Never-cut list from
docs/design/unreal/owner-checklist.md:
- first-person play with third-person cutscenes;
- the seven kinds he picked, plus "the others return";
- the whisper in the voices of the taken;
- heavy steps heard from far off;
- it follows through vents;
- 5 to 8 survivors;
- the parachute;
- the knock-out, the gun and the ship;
- a flight he steers (steer, dodge, boost, shoot);
- the holding halls and low gravity;
- the self-destruct and the pod;
- the epilogue as Kayla;
- the symbol language, with the self-destruct as its final exam;
- the puzzle setting and the reshuffle;
- manual saves plus a chapter autosave;
- graphic gore;
- two or three original songs;
- Easter eggs and the stuff on the floor;
- "not too hard".

11. [MAJOR] 6-production s3 ("SBRules depends on nothing but the engine core... tests run in seconds without a level");
2-creatures s4.4 (200 seeded runs per stretch, 10 profiles) - The creature rules depend on path distance through doors,
occlusion, light volumes, the "watched" test (on screen and lit) and the navmesh. None of these exists without a level,
so the headless simulation as written cannot run. FIX: Each Layout JSON also exports a room-portal graph: path distances,
occlusion per portal, light volumes and hide spots. SBRules simulates on that graph. Real-navmesh and rendered "watched"
checks are a few seeded functional routes per creature, run overnight. The graph's numbers are re-checked in the engine
at each milestone.

12. [MAJOR] 4-puzzles-world s1 to s2 and s9; 6-production s5 ("Other free Fab listings: props as needed") - The school is
112 x 81 m over six levels (about 54,000 m2), with a 500-seat theatre, a fly tower, bleachers and a library. About 40
puzzles need readable props whose values change with the seed: the drill log, invoices, contact sheets, the patch
diagram, the cue list, the programme. Megascans covers debris, not school furniture, and no prop source has been
checked. Every Fab addition needs the owner's own login. FIX: Before approval, write an asset census per chapter that
names a source for each hero prop: a checked free Fab listing, a Blender script, or Megascans. A puzzle whose props have
no source is simplified. Build one runtime "printed paper" system (text templates from Data/ rendered to textures), so
seeded values never need hand-made textures. Dress only the rooms on the route; the rest stay lockdown-locked. The
owner gets Fab additions as one batched list a week.

13. [MAJOR] 6-production s8 (the first slice) - The core is right: the hunter, the look, the safe room, one puzzle.
But:
- it measures only the hunter, which has its own pipeline, so the cost of one more creature (the number the schedule
  most depends on) stays unknown;
- no maul is tested, though the owner asked for graphic gore;
- "done when he says it looks and feels right" has no time box;
- the four-tier quality ladder can wait.
FIX: Add one Dark Dweller with its light-volume rule and its maul, and time it on its own. Keep only High and the
path-traced reference. Set an 8-week box: if the slice has not passed by then, review the options (a simpler look target,
hardware, scope). Log builder-days per item; that is the schedule's measuring stick.

14. [MAJOR] 5-audio s7.2 (about 3,100 lines, 2,200 Danny takes, 2 to 2.5 h of speech); 6-production s4 (phone face
capture for 30 to 40 lines) - Recording teenage friends under parents' releases at that volume is a schedule risk that
s10 does not price. The whisper bank and the Mimic calls also wait on these lines. FIX: Cap the script at about 1,200
lines:
- radio barks are systemic lines, not takes per state;
- state variants only for Danny's top 150 lines;
- the pinned AI voices are the shipping fallback for every line;
- real recordings only for cutscenes, Kayla's key radio lines and the lines the whispers are cut from, recorded in two
  batches after the script is locked at the end of the grey pass.

15. [MINOR] 6-production s2, s4, s5 and s7 - Claims checked by web search:
- confirmed: MegaLights is production-ready and Lumen Lite exists in 5.8; Megascans has been free on Fab since 5 Nov 2026
  (but listing licence text was reported as not yet updated, so keep the per-listing check); the Unreal MCP server is
  Experimental, with reports of dropped connections, so the plan is right to treat it as an extra;
- needs labelling: one-camera body capture is Experimental, with a GPU crash report on 5.8.2 and depth errors off-axis;
  MetaHuman Collections are Experimental;
- missing: Git LFS on the free plan also has a 10 GiB monthly bandwidth limit, and a clone on a new PC counts against it;
- Higgsfield: the owner asked for it by name, but the account holds 1.57 credits, its terms are unread, and the plan
  ships posters, notes, graffiti and sky cards from it.
FIX: Mark Collections and the body capture as Experimental in s4 and s5, each with its fallback. Add LFS bandwidth to s7.
Add a decision for a Higgsfield credit budget, and read the terms before the first picture that ships. Pin 5.8.x for the
whole project: 5.8 is the last planned UE5 release (checked).
