# Stay Behind: the story bible (draft 1, the writer)

What this is: the story of Stay Behind, written so it can be built from. The owner's answers in requirements.md
sections 7 and 8 are settled and are not marked. Everything else is marked: **(P1)** to **(P9)** are the lead's
working proposals, **(proposal)** is mine, and **[D1]** to **[D8]** point to the "Decisions for the owner" at the end.
All names, lines, places and creatures here are original. Patterns from the model games are named where used.

---

## 1. The premise, the themes, the title

**The premise in ten lines**
1. A cold Friday night in late November. Rovloc High is packed for the first home basketball game of the season [D6].
2. At halftime Kayla tells Danny her family is moving away in June. He makes a joke and walks out, as he always does. She follows him.
3. In the parking lot every light in the city goes out, and a ship the size of a city district settles over it.
4. A small ship lands on the football field. Something very tall crawls out of it and walks toward the school.
5. Danny and Kayla run back inside and down to the basement with Gus, the night janitor. Above them, the full building goes quiet.
6. Most of those people are not dead. They are taken, alive, up into the ship.
7. Six survivors hold out in the old fallout shelter under the school. Kayla works the radio. Danny goes up floor by floor for what they need, past a different creature on each floor and the hunter on all of them.
8. A rescue helicopter lifts them off the roof and is shot down. Kayla is taken. Danny parachutes into the dark city alone.
9. He takes an alien's gun and ship, flies up to the mothership, frees who he can and puts Kayla in an escape pod.
10. Then he stays behind and holds the self-destruct by hand. The boy who always left is the one who stays.

**The three themes**
1. **Leaving and staying.** Running is the safe move in a horror game, and it is Danny's flaw. Every chapter asks him to stay for someone. Leaving is never punished. It is just remembered.
2. **Counting people.** Ms. Kessler counts her kids. The aliens collect a city. The hidden ledger counts what Danny does. The survivors write the names of the taken on the shelter wall. The question underneath: who gets counted, and who does the counting.
3. **Voices.** What the hunter takes from people is their voices. Danny hides his real voice behind impressions and jokes. Kayla's voice on the radio is the one warm sound in the game. The last thing Danny gives away is his own voice, on purpose.

**What "stay behind" means at four points**
1. **Prologue, a joke.** Kayla's news. Danny: "So I'm the one who stays behind. Cool. Love that for me." The player hears the title said about Kayla leaving, as a throwaway line, in the first ten minutes.
2. **Chapter 1, a rule.** Ms. Kessler, as the steel door is barred: "Nobody goes up. We stay behind this door until help comes." Here staying means hiding. Danny breaks the rule every chapter, because someone has to.
3. **Chapter 5, a gift.** The helicopter has too few seats. Gus stays behind on the roof so a kid can go, and in the falling helicopter Kayla straps the only parachute onto Danny: "Go. I'm right behind you." She is the one who stays behind in the wreck.
4. **Chapter 8, a choice.** Danny holds the lock so the pods can go. In the last pod scene he tells Kayla "Go. I'm right behind you." It is her line from the helicopter, and this time it is a lie.

The epilogue turns it once more. Now Kayla is the one left behind, alive and carrying him.

**How the title's spoiler becomes a strength** (pattern: Red Dead Redemption 2 tells you early that the hero is dying, so the question becomes what he does with the time left)
- The title is first said as a joke about someone else, so for a while the player hears it as being about Kayla.
- Every chapter offers someone else who could stay behind: Kessler at the door, Ray outside it, Gus on the roof, Kayla in the wreck. The player keeps guessing who.
- In Chapter 3 the hunter marks Danny (the Mark, [D2]). From then on the player knows he will not get home before he will admit it. What is left to find out is how, for whom, and what he says.
- The last meaning is something the player does: they hold the button. The ending they expected is in their own hands.

## 2. The cast

### Danny Reyes, 16
- **Who:** the class clown. He does impressions (his Ms. Kessler is famous: chin in, flat voice, "Mister Reyes. Is something funny?"). He makes jokes at the worst moments, and he is quick, light and kind underneath. He wears a red beanie and a grey hoodie. His mother is a night-shift nurse.
- **The jokes are armour.** Rules for writing them:
  - he jokes hardest when he is most afraid;
  - he names the hunter "Stretch" and keeps it up until Chapter 3;
  - he must be really funny at least four times (the prologue twice, two Chapter 2 nights), or the armour reads as a writer's trick;
  - the jokes get shorter and darker each chapter;
  - in Chapter 3 one joke fails in front of everyone (section 8, line 9);
  - his very last line is a joke, and it lands (section 4, Chapter 8).
- **His flaw (proposal):** he leaves. When something gets hard he makes a joke and walks out of the room, the team or the conversation. Tonight he walks out on Kayla's news. In a survival game leaving is often the smart move, so every chapter tests him with it, and his arc ends when he chooses to stay.
- **What he walked away from before [D3]:** recommended, **his father**. His dad left when Danny was twelve, and Danny is afraid he is the same kind of person. The jokes started that year. The alternatives are in D3.
- **His face is seen often:** in third-person scenes, and in play in dark windows, the shelter's mirror and the trophy case glass (pattern: Red Dead 2 keeps its hero's face on screen).
- **What he carries:** the red beanie; Marcus's drumstick (from Chapter 2); Kayla's film camera (from Chapter 6); his notebook of alien symbols, whose handwriting gets worse as the Mark spreads.

### Kayla Brooks, 16 (the surname is a proposal)
- **To him:** best friend since second grade. They grew up in the same apartment block. Everyone assumes they are together. Neither has said anything, and the game never settles it (proposal).
- **Her voice:** dry, fast, amused. She is a planner, the yearbook photographer, and she carries her grandfather's mechanical film camera.
- **What she wants:** to leave this town and see more of the world. Before she goes, she wants Danny to say one true thing without a joke on it. He only does it in Chapter 8.
- **Why she is the one on the radio:** she sprains her ankle on the basement stairs in the prologue (proposal), so she cannot climb the floors. She is the best of them at Gus's old blueprints and at the school's camera feeds, so she takes the radio base. She keeps Danny company, gives directions and stops him from going quiet. Her phrase: **"Stay on the line, okay?"**, because he went silent on her once before.
- **The radio rules** (from research 01):
  - at least half her calls are not about the task in hand;
  - she is wrong once (Chapter 2), and says sorry;
  - she speaks for at most 6 seconds while something is hunting him;
  - her camera feeds die floor by floor, so her help fades as they climb.
- **Kayla in person:** the end of Chapter 1 (the code word), the Chapter 3 night (the Mark), the Mimic floor in Chapter 4, the roof and the helicopter in Chapter 5, and Chapter 8.
- **The code word:** "What time?" / "Four." It comes from their standing plan, Sal's at four. It is the only way to test a Mimic face to face.
- **(P1):** nothing ever fakes Kayla on the radio. The radio is always her.

### Ms. Kessler's judgment, chapter by chapter (she is never a villain: a frightened adult protecting "my kids" by counting them)
- **Chapter 1:** right about everything. Rations, the rota, the barred door. She keeps them alive the first night.
- **Chapter 2:** she starts deciding who "needs" medicine. Mari's inhaler is "for emergencies". Ray's painkillers are "for the children".
- **Chapter 3:** the shortwave says the helicopter comes at dawn on Monday with **five seats**. She hides it. Danny can overhear it.
- **Chapter 4:** Ray comes back after dark, knocking. She will not open the door, because "the things up there sound like people."
- **Chapter 5:** she reads out the list of who goes up to the roof. How it ends depends on SHARED truth (table above).

### Already taken when the game starts, and why they matter later

- **The announcer** (a stranger): his halftime words are the hunter's first whispers.
- **Danny's mother** is not taken: she is on shift at the hospital across the river. Her note on their apartment door is a side place in Chapter 6.

## 3. The aliens

**What holds in all three versions (proposal)**
- **The Patrol are the aliens themselves**, in grown armour. They search room by room and take people alive (P2).
- **The hunter is not one of them. It is their collector:** a tall, folded thing they keep like a hound. It stands about three metres and stoops and folds indoors (P7).
  - It takes the ones who hide or resist.
  - What it takes with its own hands it does not hand over. It carries them to its own nest on the ship and keeps their voices. That is why it whispers in them (P1).
  - Someone in the nest goes quiet within about a day and cannot be woken (proposal). This one rule lets the hunter's whisper stand for the people truly lost, and gives the hunter's catches more weight than the Patrol's.
- **The floor creatures are animals brought from other worlds:** the Dark Dwellers, Listeners, Still Ones, Mimics, Swarm and Brute. They are let loose as beaters to flush people out of hiding. They do not take; they maul (P2).
- **The taken are held asleep and whole** in long halls on the mothership, in rows of soft cradles, breathing and murmuring. Nothing graphic is ever shown. Whoever the Patrol took can be woken. The newest wake in under a minute; people taken on the first night need a symbol sequence and time.
- **The ship leaves when its hold is full.** Danny reads this in the symbols in Chapter 6, and it gives the last chapters their clock.
- **Phones (P4, proposal):** the ship's arrival sends one pulse that cooks every lithium battery in the city.
  - Phones, laptops, car keys and e-bikes swell, go hot and die. Dead phones lie everywhere: the stuff on the floor.
  - Older chemistry still works: alkaline batteries (flashlights, the school's walkie-talkies), zinc-air (Gus's hearing aid), lead-acid (the generator's starter, the old shortwave), and anything mechanical (Kayla's film camera).
  - So light and calls must be earned with scarce batteries. Maps are on paper (Gus's blueprints, fire-exit plans).
  - It also explains why the rescue comes from outside the city, using its own equipment.

**Three versions of why they came (P8) [D1]**

**A. The Harvest.** They are a long-haul people who travel between stars for generations. They stock their ships with living cargo for work and for trade further on: a city is a harvest. The halls are cargo holds: cold, numbered, sorted by size and health. The symbols Danny learns are manifests and routing marks. *In play:* puzzles are logistics (manifests, sorting gates, cargo lifts), and the Patrol checks tags. *The ending:* the self-destruct is manual because the cargo-release locks need a hand held on them, and Danny stays because of the Mark. The sadness is that he meant nothing to them. *Risk:* the most familiar of the three.

**B. The Voice Collectors (recommended).** They have no voices; they lost speech so long ago that their language is written light and tone. They take the living and keep them asleep because a sleeping mind still talks in its own language, and the ship listens. Each ship is a library of peoples: it fills, moves on, and fills again. The hunter was the first people they ever took, bred over ages into a collector, and the voices it keeps are its pay. *In play:*
- the symbol language has sounds: each symbol has a tone, panels answer to tones, and the Patrol's scanners speak them;
- the holding halls hum with thousands of sleeping murmurs, the loudest quiet place in the game;
- the Mark is the hunter holding a thread of Danny's voice, so it hears what he hears (which is how it finds the shelter);
- the core is a living lock that obeys only a command held by a living mind with a hand on it.

*The ending:* Danny's last act is to give his voice to the ship on purpose, the one thing the collectors never got freely. His command is the last thing it hears. *Why I recommend it:* it ties the stolen voices, the symbol language, the Mark and the hand-held self-destruct into one idea, and its sadness belongs to this story. *Easter egg:* in the epilogue, if Danny shared the notebook, Kayla can read the hunter's symbol. It means "the first one taken".

**C. The Return.** They were here thousands of years ago and left something in the rock under what is now the school. The 1960s fallout shelter was dug right next to it, and its back wall already carries their symbols. The small ship lands at Rovloc High because the thing below has been calling the fleet. The Mark makes Danny part of the signal. *In play:* Chapter 1 shows the old symbols, so the player learns the language from the start, and a late reveal goes back under the school. *The ending:* the self-destruct must be held because the signal stops only while a marked mind holds it. The epilogue sends Kayla down to the stone to see it gone dark. *Risk:* the most twist-shaped, it needs careful setting up in Chapter 1, and it adds a location.

## 4. The story, chapter by chapter (the shared frame; chapter names are proposals)

The time runs Friday night to Monday night. Each school chapter is about half a day.

### Prologue: "Halftime" (20 min, mostly guided)
1. **Black.** A gym crowd heard through a wall; the buzzer. The announcer: "That's the half, folks. Please remain in your seats for the halftime draw." (These words come back as the hunter's first whispers.)
2. **First person: the trophy corridor.** Kayla photographs Danny with her film camera (the flash). Marcus spins a drumstick. The player can buy a cookie, look in the trophy case (Danny's face in the glass) and read the posters. A drama club poster for "The Masque, 1989" is an Easter egg from the earlier game.
3. **Third person, cutscene 1:** walking to the back doors. "My mom took the job. We move in June." Danny jokes ("So I'm the one who stays behind") and pushes out into the cold. She follows.
4. Marcus catches up: "You guys are leaving? It's halftime." He runs back for his board: "You guys'll wait, right?" Danny: "We'll be at the gate."
5. **Play: the parking lot** (4 minutes, walking, banter). Danny tries three jokes. The second one gets a laugh out of her (section 8, line 1). She almost says something real.
6. **The pulse.** Car alarms choke off. The streetlights go dark street by street toward them, then the school's windows. Kayla's phone goes hot in her pocket; she throws it and it hisses on the asphalt. Behind the gym walls the crowd's noise turns to confusion.
7. **Cutscene 2: the mothership.** The clouds light from above. A dark shape, endless, its underside crawling with slow lights, settles over the city without a sound. Two small figures seen from behind.
8. **Cutscene 3: the small ship** drops onto the football field between them and the gate. Steam. A hatch. Something crawls out on long hands, unfolds to three metres and turns its long pale face toward the school.
9. **Play, guided:** back inside through the band-room door, not toward the gate. The halls are dark. The panic is suggested, never shown: running shadows across door glass, a scream cut off by a closing door, the PA crackling "remain calm" and dying. Kayla falls on the basement stairs (the ankle).
10. **Gus** steps out with a flashlight: "You two. Down. Now." The hunter is heard far off, heavy, then seen once at the end of a hall through wired glass, stooped under the ceiling, folding itself through a doorway.
11. **The last image:** the steel door is barred. Through its small window, the corridor light dies. In the last of it a skateboard rolls slowly across the floor on its own and stops. Cut to black, then the title.

### Chapter 1: "The Shelter" (70 min)
1. Gus's boiler room. Flashlights, the first batteries, Kayla's ankle strapped up.
2. Survivors arrive one by one, and Gus decides whether to open each time: Kessler with Owen and Mari from the music stairs; Priya down the AV cable chase; Ray, bleeding at the knee, hammering on the door.
3. The fallout shelter from 1962 (yellow-and-black signs, water drums, an old shortwave). It becomes home. **Cutscene 4:** the first count. "We stay behind this door until help comes."
4. **The generator:** fuel, a starter part, a puzzle. Lights. The camera monitors flicker on and Kayla takes the radio base. The first call: "Stay on the line, okay?"
5. **The steam tunnels** to the cafeteria storeroom for food. The hunter is heard in the pipes only.
6. **The power fails.** The Dark Dwellers come with the dark (taught while the hunter is away). Danny restarts the generator from the far fuel store.
7. The night: Mari plays the camp song (cutscene 5), and the shortwave says rescue will come "from the roofs". In the tunnels Danny hears the first familiar whisper: Marcus's "you'll... wait..." from a vent, from where the hunter really is.
8. **The turn:** at the generator, to the group, Danny makes his promise: "I'm getting everyone out. All of you. Which, historically, from me, means nothing. But." The hunter will say this back to him in Chapter 8.

*What the player learns:* the shelter's rules, the generator loop, that people were taken rather than killed (the drag marks, the empty shoes), and that the hunter speaks with voices.

### Chapter 2: "Halftime Draw", the first floor, the Listeners (55 min)
1. The gym, abandoned: coats on the bleachers, the scoreboard stuck at halftime, raffle tickets everywhere, one sneaker. The Listeners are blind and hunt by sound.
2. Kayla's radio is a danger here, so the volume knob matters (the STAYED ON THE LINE count begins).
3. The nurse's office: Gus's batteries, Mari's inhaler, painkillers. Priya's locker: her glasses. The band room: Marcus's drumstick on the floor.
4. Kayla is wrong once: from an old camera angle she sends him into the room where the Listeners nest. "I'm sorry. I'm sorry. I saw it wrong."
5. **The turn:** on a camera Priya sees the Patrol carrying sleeping people out to a small ship. They are breathing. "They're alive." Hope, and something much worse.

*Learns:* the taken are alive; the first symbols (copied from the small ship's hull into the notebook).

### Chapter 3: "Still Life", the second floor, the Still Ones (55 min)
1. The art rooms and the library. The Still Ones move only when they are not looked at. **Mari comes along** to watch one corridor while Danny crosses another: two pairs of eyes.
2. The music room: guitar strings for Mari, who laughs at the gross things he says about the room.
3. **The Mark (first person, never a cutscene) [D2]:** the hunter's whisper behind him makes him turn his head. That is the floor's trick, because the Still Ones move while he looks away. It catches him, lifts him, presses one long finger to his forearm, and puts him down. It takes Mari instead. He hears her call his name once, from far off.
4. Danny wakes alone (cutscene 6): black lines like frost on his arm, cold to the touch.
5. **The turn, in the shelter:** he must tell them. He starts with a joke and cannot finish it (section 8, line 9). Mari's cot is empty. That night Kayla limps to the generator room to sit with him (in person). He can show her the Mark or hide it (SHARED truth).

*Learns:* someone is always lost. The Mark is cold and it spreads. Kessler is hiding the "five seats" message, which Danny can overhear.

### Chapter 4: "Who's There", the third floor, the Mimics (55 min)
1. The science labs and classrooms. The Mimics look like a person from behind and call in a known voice, full and clear, and what they say is a lie (P1). The tell: they do not respond to you.
2. The cameras on this floor die. Danny goes quiet on the radio, so **Kayla limps up in person.**
3. A figure in a yellow rain jacket, ahead and facing away: "Danny, over here." He asks "What time?" It does not answer. Then the real Kayla, behind him (section 8, line 10).
4. Together they carry back the fuel and the part for the shortwave's antenna.
5. **The turn:** Ray went out alone for Caleb's letterman jacket (his proof) and comes back knocking after dark. **Cutscene 7:** Kessler will not open the door. Danny can go round through the tunnel hatch and check (does Ray answer him?) and let him in, or leave it shut. That night the Mark burns, and something tests the steel door from the other side, slowly. Kessler: "We go to the roof at first light."

### Chapter 5: "Five Seats", the fourth floor, the Swarm, then the roof (65 min)
1. Danny goes up first to clear the route. The Swarm is small things in the vents and ceilings, drawn to warmth and movement. The roof needs a signal fire and the door chain cut.
2. **The breach:** Kayla on the radio, "It's in. It's inside, Danny." The hunter followed the Mark down to the shelter. The group comes up the service stairs with Gus, and Danny understands that he led it there.
3. On the stairs Owen freezes, with the Swarm in the ceiling (a LEFT moment).
4. **The hunter's one long chase**, across the roof's ducts and skylights. The helicopter's light finds them.
5. **Cutscene 8, boarding:** Kessler reads the list (or the group chooses together, if they know about the seats). Gus stays: "I don't fly. Somebody keeps the lights on."
6. **The lift:** first person in the cabin, look around only. The city is dark below and the mothership's underside fills the sky. Song 1 plays. Kayla's hand is next to his.
7. **The hit**, mid-line. **Cutscene 9:** spinning, the crew chief down. Kayla clips the one emergency chute onto Danny: "Go. I'm right behind you." She shoves him out.
8. **First person, the fall:** the canopy opens. Below, the helicopter goes down three blocks away. On the wreck, taller than the Patrol shapes around it, the hunter lifts someone in a yellow jacket.

*How the falling helicopter has a parachute (proposal; the crash and the parachute are settled, this detail is not):* it is the crew chief's emergency chute on a rack by the door. Kayla is nearest it. She could take it herself, and she doesn't.

### Chapter 6: "The City Alone" (80 min)
1. **Landing:** a rooftop. A Patrol lamp finds him within seconds (the owner's "instantly spotted"). Run, drop, hide.
2. **The radio is silent.** He can press talk and say her name; there is only static. No other voice fills the gap.
3. The crash site: empty seats and Kayla's film camera in the wreck. He keeps it.
4. **Side places:**
   - Sal's (the pizza place of their standing plan): the chairs are still on the tables and the oven is cold.
   - Danny's building: his mother's note on the door, "At the hospital, they need everyone. Stay where you are. Love you." He writes under it: "Went to get Kayla. Back soon. D." (proposal)
   - A laundromat where a boy is hiding from a Patrol sweep. Draw the sweep off and he lives (LEFT/HELPED).
5. The hunter is rare here: a thin silhouette on a rooftop, keeping pace. Once it whispers Kayla, from up there (section 6).
6. **The knock-out:** a first-person struggle from behind, under player input, never a cutscene. Danny takes the gun (very little energy) and the scanner.
7. Reading the small ship's panel with his notebook: the ship goes up to the mothership, and **the mothership leaves when its hold is full, at the next dawn.**
8. **The turn:** he climbs in. The hatch closes on the city.

### Chapter 7: "The Climb" (20 min)
1. A clumsy lift-off between towers; the set course.
2. The pursuit: dodge, boost and shoot through the Patrol craft.
3. **The calm before the mothership:** the pursuers fall away in cloud. Song 2 plays over two or three minutes of easy steering. If the player holds the radio's talk button, Danny talks to Kayla, who cannot hear: what he never said, then a joke, then the truth. This is where he decides. (The longer you hold it, the more he says; it counts toward STAYED ON THE LINE.)
4. Out of the cloud, the mothership's belly fills the sky. He crashes into a hangar bay.

### Chapter 8: "Stay Behind", the mothership (120 min)
**The first hour:**
- The alien decks; the Patrol; the Brute, which tears hiding places open.
- The language now spoken in tones.
- **The holding halls:** rows of sleepers murmuring. Caleb is here, and the crash survivors (who boarded, by the ledger).
- A panel shows Danny's own pattern beside the hunter's: **the Mark is a beacon.** Wherever he goes, it follows, and so does anything he rides in.
- **The low-gravity spine of the ship.**
- **The hunter's nest:** Marcus and Mari, still and silent, their lips moving with its whispers. Danny cannot wake them. He puts the drumstick in Marcus's hand.

**The last hour, beat by beat:**
1. **0:00, Kayla.** She is the newest in the nest and still warm. A short waking puzzle (symbols), and her eyes open. What she can do next depends on the ledger (section 5).
2. **0:08, the survivors.** Danny wakes those in the halls. The ones who trust him help: Priya reads panels if she has seen the notebook, Ray finds Caleb, Owen keeps watch.
3. **0:15, the pod bay.** The pods are locked to the core. Only a held command at the core releases them, one by one.
4. **0:20, the talk** (cutscene 10, in its variant). He shows her the Mark, or she already knows. "If I get in one, they follow it. They follow you."
5. **0:25, the self-destruct, part one:** three symbol locks across the low-gravity spine, with the Brute in pursuit.
6. **0:45, part two:** the pods are routed. The freed board them, each on their own legs. The player is never asked to escort them.
7. **0:50, cutscene 11, Kayla's pod.** "Go. I'm right behind you." What she does next depends on the ledger: in the best version she knows it is a lie and says "Stay on the line, okay?"; in the others she believes him, or she is too dazed to know.
8. **0:53, the run to the core** as the ship starts to come apart. The hunter arrives with every habit it has learned.
9. **0:57, the final exam:** the full symbol phrase. Each correct symbol sounds its tone, and the phrase resolves into the Danny-and-Kayla motif.
10. **0:58, Danny's last act, done by the player:**
    - The player holds one button: Danny's hand pressed to the core.
    - Pods launch one at a time on a strip of lights. The Mark spreads up his arm in view.
    - If you let go, nothing is punished: the pods wait, Danny's hand slips, and Kayla says "Danny?" The player has to put it back.
    - The hunter comes in and crouches beside him, folded, and whispers its last voice (section 6). It cannot pull him off the lock without the ship dying, so it waits.
    - Kayla is on the radio for as long as the ledger allows.
    - The last pod launches. Danny's last line, to Kayla: the Kessler voice, "Miss Brooks. Is something funny?" She laughs, crying. Then white.
11. **Kayla's pod, the one environment shot:** dawn, and the mothership breaking apart over the city. The radio hisses, then goes silent.

### Epilogue: "June" (15 min, as Kayla, first person)

## 6. The hunter's voices (P1)

## Decisions for the owner (most important first)

1. **Why did the aliens come? (P8)** A: The Harvest, people as cargo. B: The Voice Collectors, who keep the living asleep for their voices. C: The Return, something left under the school long ago. **Recommend B:** it ties the stolen whispers, the symbol language, the Mark and the hand-held self-destruct into one idea.
2. **The Mark: in Chapter 3 the hunter marks Danny and lets him go.** It explains how the hunter finds him on every floor and in the city, causes the safe-room breach, and is why he cannot ride a pod home. A: yes, as written. B: no; Danny stays only because the lock must be held by hand. **Recommend A:** it gives the ending a reason the player understands while staying Danny's choice.
3. **What did Danny walk away from before?** A: his father (Dad left when he was twelve; Danny fears he is the same). B: the basketball team he quit, which plays tonight. C: Kayla, during a bad year of hers. **Recommend A:** staying behind answers the question his whole life asks, and it needs no extra scenes.
4. **Marcus (P6).** A: taken by the hunter at halftime, so his is the first familiar voice and he is found lost in the nest. B: one of the six survivors. C: left out. **Recommend A:** Danny is now the clown, two clowns would compete, and "You guys'll wait, right?" is the guilt the whole game runs on.
5. **How strong is the language?** A: mild only. B: real but rationed (strong words at about eight peaks). C: full teen swearing. **Recommend B:** it sounds real, and the strong words still hit.
6. **The evening event.** A: the first home basketball game (an announcer, a scoreboard stuck at halftime). B: a fall dance (decorations, a DJ booth). **Recommend A:** the announcer's words become the first whispers, and a gym full of noise is easy to suggest without showing a crowd.
7. **Is one survivor always lost?** A: yes, Mari is taken by the hunter in Chapter 3 in every playthrough (his "not all of them make it"). B: no, perfect play can save all six. **Recommend A:** a fixed loss makes every later choice feel real.
8. **What the hunter takes, it keeps.** Its own catches go to its nest and are lost within a day; the Patrol's can be woken. A: yes, so the whisper always means someone truly lost. B: no, anyone taken can be woken in Chapter 8. **Recommend A:** it makes the hunter worse than the Patrol, and it makes rescuing Kayla urgent.
