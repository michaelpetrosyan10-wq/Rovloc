# Stay Behind: survival systems and small mechanics

Research report 01, 21 November 2026. Source games: Amnesia: The Bunker (AB), Alien: Isolation (AI), Dead Space remake (DS), Silent Hill 2 remake (SH2), Outlast 2 (O2); Red Dead Redemption 2 (RDR2), Metro Exodus and Firewatch only for the survivor group and the radio. The rule throughout: we take patterns, never content. Each pattern below is stated in general terms, then given Stay Behind's own material.

How claims are marked: [checked: url] means a search result confirmed it. [from memory] means it did not, so treat it as a lead. Page fetching was blocked in this session (wikipedia.org and gamesradar.com would not resolve), so every "checked" claim rests on search-result text from guides, reviews and interviews, not on full pages I read.

## Key findings (read these first)

1. **The Bunker's generator is a clock, a light switch and a noise rule all at once, and that is why it works.** Fuel goes into one machine. The machine lights the map. Every light you switch on burns fuel faster. A watch you carry shows how long is left, and the watch takes a backpack slot. [checked: https://gameranx.com/features/id/467193/article/amnesia-the-bunker-generator-system-explained/ ; https://gameranx.com/features/id/467207/article/amnesia-the-bunker-how-to-save-your-game/] Its makers call it "a key element of the game and a main source of its tension". [checked: https://gamingbolt.com/amnesia-the-bunker-interview-semi-open-world-structure-stalker-and-more] *Why it matters:* our basement already has a generator and creatures that live only in the dark, so this loop fits the agreed map almost exactly. It is the strongest single system to build.

2. **The Bunker shuffles codes but keeps key places fixed.** Codes and names on the tags change each run. Where the tags lie does not, and key items stay in the same places. Ordinary pick-ups such as bullets, sticks or the gas mask can move. [checked: https://www.gamepressure.com/amnesia-the-bunker/codes-on-dog-tags/ze10cdf ; https://www.pcgamer.com/12-takeaways-after-an-hour-with-the-next-amnesia-game-which-contains-exactly-one-gun] *Why it matters:* the player asked for codes and item places that change. Fixed places with shuffled contents give him replay value without dead ends.

3. **A tool that tells you where the monster is should also give you away.** Alien: Isolation's motion tracker beeps, and the creature hears the beep when it is close. [checked: https://www.trueachievements.com/game/Alien-Isolation/walkthrough/2] Outlast 2's camera spends batteries on night vision and on its directional microphone. [checked: https://www.pcgamer.com/outlast-2-guide/] Every information tool should cost something.

4. **Saving by hand creates tension only when it is risky and a little slow.** Isolation's makers "kept coming back to the manual save". A save takes a few seconds, the player is exposed while it runs, and save points are deliberately spaced unevenly. [checked: https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/game-design-deep-dive-the-save-system-of-i-alien-isolation-i-] The Bunker has no autosave beyond a few scripted ones. On Easy it adds extra save lamps; on Normal and above you walk back to the safe room to save. [checked: https://primagames.com/tips/does-amnesia-the-bunker-have-autosave-answered ; https://www.escapistmagazine.com/what-are-the-difficulty-differences-in-amnesia-the-bunker/]

5. **None of these games relies on a health bar.** Dead Space puts health on the hero's spine, which the camera keeps in view. [checked: https://gamedeveloper.com/design/video-designing-i-dead-space-i-s-immersive-user-interface] SH2 lets you turn off every UI element and read the hero's state from the world, plus a red screen edge and the controller's light colour. Blood on his clothes darkens and dries over time. [checked: https://gamesradar.com/games/silent-hill/silent-hill-2-remake-lets-you-disable-all-ui-elements-for-the-most-immersive-experience-and-youll-be-told-where-to-go-by-visual-sound-and-character-cues ; https://gamingbolt.com/15-incredible-details-that-you-didnt-notice-in-silent-hill-2-remake] In The Bunker, a bleeding wound leaves a trail that rats and the monster can follow. [checked: https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/AmnesiaTheBunker ; https://sirusgaming.com/amnesia-the-bunker-central-bunker-walkthrough/] So an injury is information for the enemy as well as damage to you.

6. **Puzzle difficulty and fight difficulty are separate settings, and the puzzle one is locked per save.** SH2 sets combat (damage taken and dealt) and puzzles (how complex, how clear the clues) on their own sliders. Combat can be changed any time; puzzles only at a new game. [checked: https://www.pushsquare.com/guides/silent-hill-2-combat-and-puzzle-difficulty-options-explained-what-settings-should-you-choose ; https://www.escapistmagazine.com/all-silent-hill-2-difficulty-levels-explained/] This matches the player's request for a separate puzzle setting.

7. **Easy modes in this genre change systems, not just damage.** On Easy, The Bunker's generator burns longer, there are more resources, the monster is less aggressive and there are more save lamps. Custom mode offers 30+ dials. [checked: escapist link above ; https://primagames.com/tips/how-to-play-the-custom-and-shell-shock-halloween-mode-in-amnesia-the-bunker] Isolation's Novice alien is slower, less aware and easier to distract. [checked: https://www.pcgamer.com/alien-isolation-update-adds-novice-and-nightmare-difficulty-levels] Dead Space's Story mode heals the hero automatically. [checked: https://www.destructoid.com/which-difficulty-should-you-play-the-dead-space-remake-on/] *Why it matters:* the player said "i also dont want the game to be too hard". These are proven ways to keep the fear and remove the stalling.

8. **The safe room should be safe, except once.** The Bunker's office is safe while its steel doors stay locked, until the monster bores through a wall. [checked: https://www.gamepressure.com/amnesia-the-bunker/tips-and-tricks/z610ca1] One breach in a whole game is worth more than constant threat. It suits our "not all of them make it".

9. **A survivor group matters when its needs are concrete and its fate is quietly counted.** RDR2's camp has stocks of provisions, ammunition and medicine. Morale and donations rise when you contribute, and a run-down camp "feels grim and tense, foreshadowing" collapse. [checked: https://www.gamerevolution.com/guides/450813-red-dead-redemption-2-camp-needs-and-morale] In Metro Exodus, which crew members survive depends on hidden choices, and the ending depends on how many are saved. [checked: https://www.gamesradar.com/metro-exodus-good-ending-how-to-get ; https://gamepressure.com/metro-exodus/how-do-the-morality-system-work/z1be6d] This matches the player's "no meter; the game remembers".

10. **A radio companion builds a bond through distance, and fails through too much talk.** Firewatch's voice on the radio carries the whole relationship, and the distance becomes the point. Critics fault the filler chatter and the lack of a way to make her stop. [checked: https://gamesbeat.com/crafting-relationships-through-radio-in-firewatch ; https://gamedeveloper.com/design/firewatch-critical-analysis ; https://www.pcgamer.com/best-writing-2016-firewatch]

## 1. Inventory and carrying

**What the games do**
- AB: few slots, bags found in the world add more, and one chest in the safe room holds the rest. The generator watch costs a slot. [checked: https://www.pcgamer.com/12-takeaways-after-an-hour-with-the-next-amnesia-game-which-contains-exactly-one-gun ; gameranx save link]
- DS: 12 slots to start, and only new suit tiers add space. Bench upgrades do not. Storage lives at the store. [checked: https://screenrant.com/increase-inventory-slots-dead-space-remake/ ; https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/how-easily-upgrade-inventory-space-dead-space-remake]
- AI: crafting parts are capped per type. You choose what to build, not what to carry. [from memory]
- In every case the choice that hurts is "leave something good behind or come back for it later".

**For Stay Behind**
- Danny's **school backpack: 8 slots**, plus **2 pocket slots** for the light and one throwable. A bigger pack found on the third floor (a hiker's bag in lost-and-found, or a teacher's gym bag) raises it to 12. That is the only upgrade, which keeps the weight "medium".
- What takes room: batteries stack 4 to a slot. A fuel can takes 2 slots, and is deliberately awkward. Medicine 1, parts stack 5, throwables 1 each.
- **Keys, notes and symbol pages go in a separate binder and never cost a slot.** Puzzle items in the backpack cause stalls for no fear.
- **The safe-room stash is local**: one cabinet per safe room. The basement also has the **kitchen dumbwaiter**: from a lit kitchen on any floor, Danny can send items down to the survivors' cabinet (a one-way delivery, and the dumbwaiter is loud). The link is in the world and gives him a reason to visit kitchens.
- Opening the backpack never pauses the game. DS's holographic menus do not stop play, and that is part of the fear. [checked: https://www.thegamer.com/dead-space-horror-ui/] Offer a "pause while in backpack" option only on the easiest setting.

## 2. Resources and their economy

**What the games do**
- O2: batteries are the master resource. Night vision costs less than the microphone, so players learn to use short bursts. [checked: https://www.pcgamer.com/outlast-2-guide/ ; https://www.mic.com/articles/175171/outlast-2-battery-guide-how-and-where-to-find-the-games-most-important-resource]
- AB: revolver ammunition comes almost one bullet at a time and loads one round at a time. [checked: https://www.pcgamer.com/12-takeaways-after-an-hour-with-the-next-amnesia-game-which-contains-exactly-one-gun ; https://www.godisageek.com/reviews/amnesia-the-bunker-review] The flashlight never runs out, but you have to wind it, and the winding makes noise. [checked: same PC Gamer link]
- AB: fuel can be topped up from a drum in the fuel store. Guides disagree on whether that drum is endless. [checked: https://www.gamepressure.com/amnesia-the-bunker/tips-and-tricks/z610ca1 ; gameranx generator link]
- DS's pacing director shapes encounters and moods, not just spawns. [checked: https://www.ea.com/technology/news/inside-dead-space-4-the-intensity-director] Item drops weighted toward what you lack are an older Dead Space habit. [from memory]

**The tuning principle: "nearly empty, never empty for long"**
- Every critical resource needs a **free fallback that costs danger instead of supplies**:
  - Light: Danny's wind-up torch (from the physics room's demo kit, or a camping torch in the janitor's store). It makes a whirring noise and gives a short burn.
  - Fuel: a slow siphon from the buses in the bus garage, or from the groundskeeper's mower shed, far from the safe room.
  - Medicine: rest in the safe room heals slowly, but costs generator time.
- **Pity rule**: when the player is at zero of something critical, the next two containers he searches are weighted toward it. Never let the player see this rule.
- **Scarcity check per area**: aim for a player arriving with 1 to 2 units of each resource and leaving with 1 to 2. Log playtests and tune from the logs.
- When the player runs dry, the game gets **harder but never stuck**. Darkness brings the Dark Dwellers closer; no throwables means hiding instead of distracting. No puzzle should ever need a resource the player can run out of.

## 3. The generator loop (AB in detail), and our basement

**How AB works**
- **Fuel to ticks**: each bottle or can adds two ticks. Refuelling just before the needle crosses a tick can stretch one fuel. [checked: gameranx generator link]
- **Burn rate**: every light you switch on drains fuel faster. [checked: https://gamerjournalist.com/how-to-avoid-the-monster-in-amnesia-the-bunker/ ; gamepressure tips] On Hard, a full tank lasts 13:45 and one can lasts 1:22. [checked: https://gamersdecide.com/articles/amnesia-the-bunker-difficulty]
- **The watch** is synced to the fuel gauge and takes a slot. [checked: gameranx save link]
- **Saving**: the save lantern sits in the safe room. A late-game claim that saving costs fuel comes from one player. [checked as a forum claim only: https://www.gamepressure.com/amnesia-the-bunker/tips-and-tricks/z610ca1]
- **Light and the creature**: sources disagree. Some say it fears light; others say the generator makes it appear less often unless you are loud. Noise always draws it. [checked as disputed: gamepressure tips ; gamerjournalist link] Its makers confirm the generator as the core tension and the monster as "ever-present". They say nothing on its exact response to light. [checked: gamingbolt interview]
- **Map structure**: one hub (the office, with the general map, the stash and the save). Each area has its own wall map behind a locked door, and finding one adds that area to the hub map. [checked: gameranx save link ; gamepressure walkthrough https://www.gamepressure.com/amnesia-the-bunker/walkthrough/z710ca2] Locked places are opened physically, with explosives, bricks or a shotgun. [checked: tvtropes link]

**For Stay Behind: the Basement chapters**
- **The generator** is the school's standby set in the boiler room, next to the survivors' room (the old caretaker's workshop, behind a steel door). Its gauge is a needle with marked ticks. A fuel can adds two ticks.
- **The stopwatch**: Danny takes the coach's stopwatch from the PE office and sets it to the gauge. It **beeps at 60 seconds left**. The beep can be heard nearby (pattern from finding 3); clicking it to silent hides the warning. That is a small choice he makes every run.
- **Light rule (our own, stated plainly so it can be learned)**: Dark Dwellers cannot enter lit space. Lit space is the rooms where Danny has turned the wall switch on while the generator runs, plus the cone of his torch. They gather at the edges of the light and wait. Each lit room raises the burn rate. The torch pushes them back only in its cone and only while it has charge.
- **Noise trade**: the running generator **masks Danny's footsteps** in the basement. When it stops, the silence is total, and his steps carry. Darkness and loudness arrive together. That is the scare.
- **When fuel runs out**: lights fade over about 4 seconds, never instantly. The emergency exit signs stay lit on their own batteries for that chapter: small green safe islands that also show the way back. Later in the game those batteries die. The player learns to notice that.
- **Locked places**: the basement map comes in parts (the boiler side, the old tunnels, the bus garage, the kitchen undercroft). Each opens with a different physical solution: a jammed fire door forced with the janitor's pry bar (loud), a padlock with a shuffled code found on a staff locker tag, a flooded section drained by working the pump (costs fuel), a bricked arch broken with a sledgehammer (very loud; it brings the hunter).
- **Survivors tie into it**: the room's light, heat and the radio base all draw on the generator. When fuel is low the room goes cold and quiet, and the survivors' lines change ("we're down to the battery lamp"). Bringing fuel back is visible care.
- **Balance**: Normal gives about 12 minutes per full tank; Easy about 18. Hardest setting: a timer that also counts down while Danny is in the safe room. Easy keeps it frozen there.

## 4. Crafting and improvised tools

**What the games do**
- AI: recipes are learned from blueprints found in the story. Distractions include the noisemaker and the flare; the flashbang works on humans but not the creature. Only fire, and loud blasts, drive the creature off for a while. A loud tool also calls it in. [checked: https://www.gamerevolution.com/guides/65748-alien-isolation-crafting-guide ; https://gamepressure.com/alien/crafting/z96a11 ; https://steamcommunity.com/app/214490/discussions/0/617329150703357934] Players say the creature grows wary of the flamethrower over time. [from memory]
- AI: rewire panels change the station itself (lights, cameras, vents, alarms). That is remote crafting. [checked: https://primagames.com/news/alien-isolation-mission-12-synthetic-solution-security-access-tuner-grid-purge-emp-mine]
- AB: problems have physical answers (bricks, explosives, fire, gas), and the noise each one makes is its price. Two cloths make a bandage. [checked: tvtropes link ; sirusgaming link]

**For Stay Behind: the rule first**
- Crafting uses **game logic only**: named school objects snap together on a simple bench (tape + kitchen timer + biscuit tin = "rattle"). **Never show real chemistry steps or real recipes for fire or blasts.** The game must not work as a how-to. The chemistry lab gives finished things (glow sticks, a cooler of dry ice from a demo, safety goggles), not formulas.
- Recipes come from **found sources inside the school**: a shop teacher's project sheet, a stagehand's checklist, a student's science-fair poster. Each is our own content and doubles as story.

**What a 16-year-old could plausibly find or build** (none can kill; per the map, all distract, light, hide or scare off)

| Source | Item | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Kitchen | Wind-up kitchen timer in a biscuit tin | Thrown noisemaker; ticks, then rings after 5 s. Best against the Listeners |
| Kitchen | Pans and lids | Bang for a big noise you make in place (calls everything; a sacrifice move) |
| Kitchen | Walk-in freezer | Cold hiding place against the Swarm |
| Dance-night leftovers (on the floor) | Glow sticks | Light that is not a torch and runs on nothing: keeps a Dark Dweller off a doorway for about 90 s; throwable trail marker |
| Janitor's store | Road flares, mop, wedges, duct tape, pry bar, keys | Flare is a one-use scare-off for the hunter; mop or wedge jams doors; pry bar forces doors loudly |
| Janitor's store | "Wet floor" sign + string | Trip-alarm: a clatter tells you something walked through |
| Workshop | Wire, tape, a dynamo crank from the physics demo kit | Wind-up torch; a crank charger for batteries (slow, noisy) |
| Theatre | Fog machine canister | Smoke for breaking sight (works on the Still Ones' rule? Designer's call; see section 8) |
| Theatre | Strobe, sound desk, rope, trapdoors | Rewire-style remote distractions; trap routes under the stage |
| Yearbook / media room | Camera with flash | Blinds a Mimic for 3 s; one charge per battery |
| Science | Dry ice in a cooler; thermal camera | Cold lure and mask against the Swarm; thermal view finds the Swarm in vents |
| Nurse's office | Gauze, tape, painkillers, a cold pack | Healing; the cold pack doubles as a Swarm mask |
| Fire points | Extinguisher | Close-range blast: a cloud plus a cold burst. Scares the hunter off once, then empty |
| Office | PA and bell panel | Rings bells or plays a tone in another wing: our rewire pattern |

**The hunter's scare-off budget**: flares and the extinguisher drive it away for a while, and it remembers. If the same item is used twice in one chapter, it closes in faster the second time (an adaptive pattern, our own numbers).

## 5. Health and injury without a HUD

**What the games do**: health on the hero's body (DS). [checked] The world and the controller show state, and drying blood shows time (SH2). [checked] A wound is a trail for enemies (AB). [checked] Bandages are a pick-up, and the hero mends lighter wounds over time (O2). [checked: https://www.magicgameworld.com/outlast-2-how-to-find-batteries-find-bandages-and-heal-guide; the healing over time is from memory]

**For Stay Behind** (first person, so the body has to show up in the hands and the sound)
- **Three states, no number**: Fine / Hurt / Badly hurt.
  - Hurt: breathing heavier; a red-brown sleeve on the arm you see when he opens doors.
  - Badly hurt: a limp (slower walk, no sprint), his hand presses on walls as he moves, his breathing can be heard by Listeners at short range, and he drips blood (the hunter can follow fresh drips for a time).
- **Bandaging takes time** (about 4 s, hands on screen) and can be interrupted. Painkillers steady his aim and hands for a few minutes but do not heal.
- **A cheap fitness band on his wrist** (a 2026 teenager would have one): glancing at it shows heart rate and fear. It **buzzes** when his pulse spikes, and a Listener can hear the buzz (finding 3). Danny can take it off, which costs him the reading. This is our version of DS's health on the body.
- **Death scenes** are where the agreed graphic horror goes. Keep them short and skippable after the first time; DS's options include skipping graphic scenes. [checked: https://ea.com/able/news/accessibility-and-player-choice-in-dead-space]

## 6. Saving and safe rooms

**For Stay Behind**
- **The save object**: a **logbook and desk lamp** in each safe room. Danny writes one line (it takes about 3 s, the pen scratching), and the line is a short diary beat that changes with the chapter and the choices made. You cannot save while a creature is hunting you nearby: the lamp flickers and he stops.
- **Where**: the agreed map says manual saves "in safe rooms". With a hunter on every floor, **each floor needs at least one small safe room** (a staff toilet with a lock, a book store, the theatre's projection booth), not only the basement. Spacing: 15 to 25 minutes of play apart on Normal, deliberately uneven (the Isolation pattern), with an extra lamp in the middle on Easy.
- **Chapter autosaves** as agreed, plus two fairness rules:
  - A **"suspend and quit" save** that is deleted when loaded, so he can stop for dinner without losing progress or gaining a free retry.
  - On Easy only, **after the third death in the same stretch**, offer a single checkpoint at the last doorway.
- **Safe-room breach**: once in the school part, scripted, as in finding 8. The room is never fully trusted again: the survivors move, and a later choice decides who carries what.

## 7. Map and finding the way

**What the games do**: SH2's map marks itself in the hero's pencil (circles, scribbles, crossed-out doors), and white cloth flags things you can use. [checked: https://pcgamer.com/games/horror/silent-hill-2-review ; https://www.escapistmagazine.com/all-silent-hill-2-difficulty-levels-explained/] AB builds its hub map from area maps found behind locked doors. [checked: gameranx save link] DS has a line on the floor that points to your goal. [from memory]

**For Stay Behind**
- **Each floor's map is its fire-evacuation plan**, taken off the wall (every school has them by the stairs). Taking it adds the floor to Danny's map, folded into the binder.
- **Danny marks the map in his own hand**: an X on a locked door, a circle on a puzzle place, a scribble on a door jammed shut. **Kayla's notes come in a different ink**, added when she tells him something over the radio. You can see whose knowledge is whose.
- **Signs in the world do the rest**: room numbers, painted floor lines in the PE corridor, "EXIT" signs on battery power (section 3), handwritten survivor arrows in chalk.
- **No floor guide line.** Option: on Easy, Kayla repeats the current goal when Danny presses "radio".
- **Marking**: Danny can chalk a mark on a door (a limited stick of chalk from any classroom). The chalk stays, and so do the Dark Dwellers' scratches over it on the way back.

## 8. Hiding, doors, light and noise

**What the games do**
- AI: hide in lockers and lean out to peek. If you stay too long, the creature comes back to where it last heard you and checks lockers; holding your breath is the last resort. Lockers also narrow your view and your options. [checked: https://www.gamepressure.com/alien/how-to-survive-on-hard/zf6a7a]
- O2: barrels, beds, and water and tall grass outdoors. Night vision gives no light away. [checked for barrels and the light: pcgamer O2 guide; beds and water from memory]
- AB and the earlier Amnesia games: doors open by degrees with the mouse. [from memory]

**For Stay Behind: each floor changes which currency matters**

| Floor creature | Costs you | Free | Hide how |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dark Dwellers (basement, power off) | darkness | noise (while the generator runs) | stay in light; glow sticks; the generator |
| Listeners | noise | light | anywhere quiet; hold breath; walk on carpet, not lino; avoid broken glass |
| Still Ones | looking away | noise | hiding is weak; keep line of sight; reverse along walls; does a mirror or phone screen count as looking? Decide one way and teach it once |
| Mimics | trust | light | do not answer a call; check with Kayla on the radio before going to a voice; camera flash shows the real face |
| Swarm (vents, ceilings) | warmth and movement | sound | move slowly; cold rooms; the freezer; dry ice; the thermal camera shows them |
| Hunter (anywhere) | everything a little | nothing | lockers, under the stage, the trophy cabinet room. Checks the last noise; tracks fresh blood |
| Brute (mothership) | hiding places themselves | n/a | hiding only buys seconds; it tears covers open, so moving is the answer |

- **Price of hiding too long**: Danny's breath grows louder after about 40 s; holding it costs a short stamina bar. His fitness band buzzes if his pulse spikes. The hunter returns to where it last heard a sound after a set delay.
- **Doors**: open by degrees (mouse or stick); peek through the wired glass panel or the gap; **hold a door shut** against a push (a struggle with a fail state); **wedge** it with a chair or a mop (one use, and it creaks when tested); **lock it behind you** with the janitor's keys (the lock is loud).

## 9. Difficulty for "not too hard"

**Three separate dials, shown as three presets with "custom"**

| Dial | What changes | When it can change |
|---|---|---|
| Creatures | hunting senses (hearing range, sight cone), speed, how often the hunter visits, grab escape time, scare-off length | any time |
| Survival | supplies found, generator burn, save lamp count, the pity rule's strength, slow healing on/off (the Story-style auto-heal of DS) | any time |
| Puzzles | number of steps, how clear the clues are, Kayla's hint ladder (see the radio section), symbol pages pre-translated or not | at a new game, or step down only at a chapter start (codes regenerate per chapter) |

- Presets:
  - **"Story"** (generous fuel, auto-heal, slower creatures, more lamps)
  - **"Survivor"** (the intended one: the numbers in this report)
  - **"Stay Behind"** (unlocked after finishing; less of everything, as AB's Shell Shock did)
- Name it all in plain words, with no "Easy" stigma. Recommend "Survivor" to the player at the start, with an option for Story.

## The survivor group (5 to 8 people, one safe room)

**Patterns that work**: concrete stocks that you top up and that change the room's mood (RDR2); hidden counting of help, with survival decided by it (Metro Exodus); crew whose talks between missions carry the story (Metro's train). [checked: links in findings 9]

**For Stay Behind (keep it at 6)**
- **Each person has one job, one need and one story.**
  - Jobs: the janitor (keys, repairs, the tunnels), the teacher (first aid, keeping order), a tech student (radio base and CCTV), plus three more.
  - Need: one named object (an inhaler, a specific medicine, glasses, a photo from a locker).
  - Story: told in two or three late-night talks at the generator.
- **Supplies are visible, not counted**: food tins on the shelf, the medicine box, the space heater on or off. There is no bar on screen.
- **Requests come with a chapter-long deadline, not a real-time clock.** If Danny fails one, the person's state changes visibly (sick, angry, gone quiet). Their survival at the helicopter becomes less likely. **Progress never stops.**
- **Quiet choices with a cost**:
  - Medicine Danny could use himself, or bring back.
  - Telling the group the truth about the taken, or not.
  - Taking the last fuel to finish a trip, or leaving it for the room's heat.
- **Who makes the helicopter** follows from that record. The ones who don't make it are lost off screen (per the agreed rule), told through an empty chair or a note.
- **Payback**:
  - Help you can see: the janitor oils the door Danny uses most, so it stops creaking.
  - The tech student restores a CCTV camera.
  - The teacher leaves a bandage pack in the dumbwaiter.
- **Keep the cast small** so the writing, the voice work and the animation for third-person scenes stay doable in an 8 to 10 hour game. Six people with two or three scenes each is about 15 scenes.

## Kayla on the radio

**What works**: distance as intimacy; the voice carries the relationship; she is with you while you are alone. [checked: Firewatch links] **What fails**: filler chatter, talking over tense moments, no way to make her stop, telling the player the answer.

**For Stay Behind**
- **The radio is physical**: a school walkie-talkie on Danny's chest strap, with a volume knob. **Her voice can be heard by Listeners and the hunter.** Turning it down is a choice with a price, since he may miss a warning.
- **She speaks only in safe or calm moments**, except short warnings. Rule: no line longer than 6 seconds while a creature is hunting Danny.
- **She is the motion tracker**: in the safe room she has the school's CCTV monitors. She can see some cameras and say "it's in the east stairs". Cameras die floor by floor, so her help fades as the game goes up. Mimics make her doubt what she sees.
- **Hint ladder**, tied to the puzzle dial: after about 4 minutes stuck, she asks what he's seeing; after 8, she remembers something about the room; after 12, she gives a strong hint. Never the full solution on the middle setting; on the lowest setting, yes if asked.
- **She can be wrong**, and she says sorry when she is.
- **The silence after she is taken** is the beat that sells the second half. Do not fill it with another radio voice. The hunter whispering in her voice **once** over the dead radio is the strongest use of the agreed whisper idea, but see the risks below.

## Risks to the agreed map

1. **Safe rooms on every floor.** "Manual saves in safe rooms" works only if every floor has one. Basement-only saving, with the hunter loose on every floor, will feel too hard for this player. Needs: a small safe room per floor, or a save lamp per floor. A decision for the lead, or for him if in doubt.
2. **Phones.** A 2026 sixteen-year-old has a phone with a torch, a camera and maps. The story must say why they fail (an alien pulse? no power? taken?). Otherwise the battery economy and the radio make no sense. Ask him. This is a story decision.
3. **Mimics versus the hunter's whisper.** Both use known voices. Without a clear difference, players will confuse them. Suggestion: Mimics call from in front (a body you can see, from behind), while the hunter whispers only from out of sight, distorted. Or the hunter is the only one that uses the voices of the taken, and Mimics use the voices of the people still alive. Needs a decision.
4. **The generator timer and "not too hard".** The Bunker's timer is famously stressful. Steam players complain about its randomness and fuel supply. [checked: https://steamcommunity.com/app/1944430/discussions/0/3808405578491327784] Limit the timer to the basement chapters, freeze it in the safe room on Easy, and give one slow free fuel source.
5. **Reshuffled item places.** Shuffle codes and ordinary pick-ups; keep key items in fixed places, or in a small set of hand-checked spots. Fully random key places would cause dead ends, and walkthroughs would no longer help him.
6. **"Nothing can be killed in the school", and the scare-off tools.** The extinguisher and flares must scare, never hurt. Keep their animation clear about this, or players will read them as weapons.
7. **Real-world how-to.** The crafting must stay game logic (see section 4), since a school's chemistry lab is a tempting source and the game must never teach real recipes.
