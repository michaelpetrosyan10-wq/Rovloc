## 2. The aliens (owner: story; the owner chose the Voice Collectors)

- **Who they are:** they lost speech long ago; their language is light and tone, and every glyph has a tone. They
  keep the living asleep because a sleeping mind still talks and the ship listens. The ship fills, moves on, fills
  again; this one leaves full at **Tuesday dawn**.
- **The Patrol are the aliens themselves**, in grown armour; their calls are tones from scanners and armour.
- **The hunter** is the first people they ever took, bred into a collector and paid in voices. Its own glyph means
  "the first one taken".
- **The floor creatures** are animals let loose to flush people out. They maul; they never take.
- **The Mark** (Ch3, the theatre stage): the hunter holds a thread of Danny's voice and hears what he hears. It is how
  it finds him, why it breaks into the shelter in Ch5, and why he cannot ride a pod ("If I get in one, they follow
  it"). Black frost-lines on his forearm, cold, spreading.
- **The core** obeys only a command held by a living mind with a hand on it. His last act gives his voice freely.
- **Taken** means alive, asleep and whole in a frosted cradle; never shown graphically. The Patrol's catches go to
  **the Holds**: all can be woken (the newest in under a minute; first-night ones need a symbol sequence and time).
  The hunter's catches go to **the Nest**, where after **about two days** a person is too far gone to wake: Marcus and
  Mari, always. Anyone there less than two days can be woken: Kayla, and Ray, Owen or Gus if the hunter took them (CK:
  the plain result of the two-day rule; story writes each as a short waking step).

## 13. The gun, the scanner, the flight, low gravity (owner: systems; self-destruct: world)

- **The gun** (from the city's midpoint): **six cells**, glowing; the charge whine tells what is left. Quick bolt, 1
  cell: staggers a soldier 4 s, scatters Swarm or a Dweller, drives the hunter back 30 s. Charged (hold 1.5 s), 3
  cells: kills a soldier, drives the hunter off 90 s, staggers the Brute 3 s. Never kills the hunter. Shot 90 dB,
  heard a block away. Recharge: a pylon, 20 s for 3 cells (a hum the Patrol notices within 30 m), or a cartridge (2).
  The first takedown is by hand; later ones, a bolt to the neck plate, 1 cell, 50 dB.
- **The scanner:** a ping shows warm life within 25 m for 4 s through walls; **each ping spends one of the gun's
  cells** (a cartridge refills two), then 20 s before the next; a 40 dB chirp the Patrol hears; reads glyph locks
  (free); maps a deck once linked at a node. There is no other locator, and nothing locates for free or on a cooldown
  alone (checklist line 107; whether he wants a locator at all is put to him, systems Open).
- **The flight** (18 min, six segments, a checkpoint each except on Stay Behind (section 11); CK split): lift-off and the hunter's hand (2), the
  rooftops (3), the pack and three danger-lane forks (4), the cloud with Song 2 and the talk button (3), the fins (4),
  the hangar mouth and the crash (2). No canyon, no bridge; the city a far field. Corridor about 80 x 40 m. Dodge 0.4 s
  (1.2 s cooldown); boost 3 s, refills in 6; shooting overheats after 3 s, cools in 2; a lock tone 1.5 s before a
  missile (Story 2.5); 4 hits (Story 6); scraping costs a hit, never a death.
- **Low gravity** (the Spine, **about 15 min**): push off where he looks, drift, grab; the gun as a thruster, 1 cell;
  down changes once; thin air.
- **The self-destruct** (about 40 min, world): Spine locks > route the pods > the core exam > the run to the pod bay >
  Kayla's pod > **the hold**, one hand on the pod release. Letting go is never punished.
