PROCESSING CHAINS (tools/finish.py, tools/voxfx.py; exact parameters)
DIALOGUE  pick: lowest recogniser WER, then (tagged lines) most non-word sound, then the take needing the least
          tempo change; retakes from tools/retake.py (a take misheard after processing is excluded, or used
          without the tempo change if it is inside the gate without it).
          trim: lead 0.06 s, trail 0.20 s around frames within 40 dB of the loudest 10 ms frame; fade in 5 ms,
          fade out 30 ms (squared).  pauses inside the line > 0.40 s (frames 40 dB under the loudest) cut to
          0.34 s with a 10 ms cross-fade.  rate: words / energy span and words / recogniser word span (tagged
          lines: laugh time and word gaps > 0.3 s taken out); if either is outside 2.2-3.3 words/s, ffmpeg
          atempo (pitch kept) to 3.25 (or up to 2.3).  loudness: -20 LUFS integrated (BS.1770, pyloudnorm), true
          peak <= -1.5 dBTP (look-ahead limiter on the 4x-oversampled envelope, ceiling -1.8 dBTP, attack 2 ms,
          release 80 ms, then re-normalised).  MP3: ffmpeg libmp3lame VBR -q:a 4, mono, 24 kHz, no metadata.
GUS       after the pause cut: WORLD (pyworld harvest/cheaptrick/d4c, 5 ms frames) pitch -1 st, formants x0.97.
GUS DEEP  the same with pitch -3 st, formants x0.93 (Gus C; at -1 st it measured 109-118 Hz and 0.86 to Marcus A).
PA        Butterworth band-pass 300-3,800 Hz (4th order), peak-normalise, tanh drive 1.4, hall slaps at 140 / 330
          / 560 ms at -15 / -20 / -25 dB, noise reverb tail (0.9 s, decay constant 0.22 s, gain 0.012), trim
          (lead 0.03 s, trail 0.4 s, 45 dB), -20 LUFS, -1.5 dBTP. No rate gate (two words and an echo).
MONSTER   base take trimmed (lead 0.02, trail 0.1) = "clean". Presets (voxfx.PRESETS):
          M1 pitch -2.0 st, formants x0.94, slower x1.10, pitch range x0.85, whisper double -15 dB at +30 ms,

          M2 -3.5 st, x0.90, x1.15, range x0.70, whisper -11 dB at +35 ms, sub -18 dB, comb wet 0.18, jitter 0.25 st,
             presence +2 dB
          M3 -6.0 st, x0.84, x1.22, range x0.45, whisper -7 dB at +40 ms, sub -14 dB, comb wet 0.25, jitter 0.6 st,

          (whisper double: same words unvoiced, formants x1.04 of the preset, high-pass 400 Hz; sub: pitch a further

9. WHAT IS WEAK
- No one has listened. Every choice in the closing pass was made by measurement (recogniser, pitch, speaker
  embedding). Danny B, Priya B and Gus A are now voices DESIGNED by WORLD shifts of a built-in voice; they may sound
  less natural than a plain built-in voice. The player's ears decide.
- Kokoro's own card says part of its training data is synthetic audio from closed commercial TTS, and several of
  its voice names match commercial voices. We removed sky, bella and onyx, but af_sarah (Priya A), bm_george and
  bm_lewis (Marcus B, Gus B) are still names on the reviewer's list. If the lead wants zero name-match risk, those
  three candidates need recasting too (the same probe tool does it in minutes).
- atempo changed 18 lines by 1.5-19 %: WSOLA can sound a little phasey on breathy takes.
- Some pairs are still close: Gus A vs Gus C 0.94 (same base voice, different engine), Danny A vs Danny C 0.86 (by
  design: same blend, engine comparison), Gus B vs Marcus B 0.80 (both bm_lewis), Danny B vs Marcus A 0.69.
- Frightened lines are held to <= 3.6 words/s; real panic is faster.
- Name bank: 10 names; "Alex" comes out as "Alec" on 3 of 4 takes; names outside the bank fall back to no name.
- The room-tone bed is a new sound in monster files (36 dB under the line); the game's ambience may make it
  unnecessary.
- Chatterbox runs on a 4-bit language model for speed; it was not compared by ear with the 8-bit one. No Perth
  watermark.
- The recogniser gate is Whisper small.en: it can accept a slightly mumbled word and it once rejected a correct
  "Forty" spelled "Fourty" (alias added).
