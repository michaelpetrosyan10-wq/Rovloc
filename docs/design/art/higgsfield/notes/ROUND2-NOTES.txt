PROPORTIONS (target, in head-heights; "head" = crown to the tip of the face)
The pictures all draw the head TOO BIG (S3 v2 reads 6.3 heads tall; P1/P2 about 4.5-5.5 because the long face
counts). Do not copy that: the player's rule is a small narrow head high on a long neck, about 1/12 of his height.
- Total height 11 studs (rig) = 12 heads; one head = 0.92 studs (about 33 cm at 4 m).
- Face width at the eye holes = 0.36 head (the face is about 2.7 times as long as it is wide, as in B3).
- Skull: small, round and narrow at the back; the cranium is only the top 0.4 of the head; the face hangs 0.6 below it.
- Neck: 0.8-1.0 head long, as thick as 0.25 head (a wrist), tendons and the trachea showing; it enters the skull
  from below and BEHIND (HEAD profile), so the face juts forward-down like a prow ahead of the throat.
- Shoulders at about 9.7 heads from the floor (0.81 of height); shoulder width only 2.3 heads (narrow, bony knobs).
- Crotch at 6 heads (legs = half his height). Knees at about 3.2 heads (2.98 studs on the rig).
- Arms hanging: fingertips at about 2.4 heads from the floor (2.2 studs on the rig), i.e. below the knees.
- Hand (wrist to middle fingertip) = 1.4 heads: the hand is LONGER than the whole head (B3 shows it; keep it).
  Fingers are about 60 % of the hand; each finger has knotted, swollen knuckles; five fingers, a long thin thumb.
- Ribcage about 2.5 heads long; sunken belly; sharp hip bones; spine knobs and shoulder blades show on the back (BODY back view).

MATERIALS
- Face: old ivory gone leathery - pale warm ivory (about RGB 205,194,166 is right), finely wrinkled all over like dry
  skin, NOT glossy glaze; a deeply furrowed brow (horizontal wrinkles, B3); a faint ridge down the middle where a nose
  would be, no nostrils (the HEAD front view shows a nostril mark: leave it out). Roughness high (0.7-0.8), a little
  subsurface, a soft sheen only on the brow and the point. Micro-wrinkle normal map.
- Eye holes: huge, slanted (outer corner up), almond, set high and wide; pure glossy black depth (they read as holes
  in the face, not eyeballs); ONE tiny pale pinprick of light deep in each (the rig's Neon pupils: keep them tiny;
  the right one slightly larger is fine). P3 shows what happens if they are too big or bright: it becomes a cartoon.
- Two dark streaks: thin, straight, from the inner/lower lid of each eye hole down the face, like dried run marks,
  darker grey-brown, not red, never wet.
- Mouth: a small closed slit near the tip of the face, no lips, no teeth showing.
- Hair: thin, flat, dark, matted, only on the back/top of the small skull (a cap seen in profile); small flat human
  ears set low and far back, half in the hair. No hat ever.
- Skin of the body: pale grey-ivory, thin, dry, tight over bone; blue-grey veins on the chest, belly, forearms and
  backs of the hands; tendons in the neck and forearms; pores and fine hairs. Living skin over bone - never bare bone
  (S2 v1 and the round-1 skeleton test are what to avoid).
- Hands: old, leathery, veined, deep creases on the palm, short plain yellowish human nails - NO claws, no talons.
- Cloth: what is left of Danny's own clothes - a grey cotton hoodie with white drawstrings split along the shoulder,
  side and back seams, the sleeves stopped at the elbows, the hem above the ribs, the hood torn and hanging behind the
  neck (never over the head: it hides the face, see P4 v1); faded blue jeans ending at mid-shin, burst along the side
  seams, frayed; bare feet. Cotton with weave and stress folds, denim with fading at the knees. The round-2 sheets
  tidy this into a vest and shorts - keep it torn.

WHAT MUST NOT BE LOST FROM B3
1. The long narrow face that tapers to a point far below where a chin would be (the scariest single thing).
2. Huge black slanted eye holes with only a pinprick of light: a blank starved face that looks at you.
3. The two dark streaks and the wrinkled brow (they make it old and sad, not alien).
4. The big old human hand, longer than the face, reaching into frame first.
5. The way he is shown: out of the dark, from above, leaning in, lit from behind or by the torch only, too still.
Things to keep OUT: a beak or plague-doctor look (P1 v1, P4 v2 drift toward it: keep the face flat-fronted and
leathery, not a hard horn), a big round or egg head, a "grey alien" (no huge bulbous cranium: HEAD's crown is already
a little too wide), glowing eyes, bare bones, a stick-pole neck (P1 v2), anything red near the face.

IS IT THE SAME BEING FROM PICTURE TO PICTURE?
Yes for the face in P1 v3, P2 v2, P4 v2 and the HEAD sheet (long tapering pale face, black slanted holes, streaks,
small slit mouth). P3 is a cousin (the face reads like draped cloth with two bright eyes). The BODY sheet v2 has
the right body and the wrong face. Generated pictures cannot hold the identity by themselves - the model must.
