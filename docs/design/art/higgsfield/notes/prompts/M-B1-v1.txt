Dark, underexposed horror film still, real 35mm photograph. A long American high school corridor at night seen down its length from a low camera: steel lockers on both walls, ceiling tiles, linoleum with a wet shine, one flickering fluorescent tube at the far end, haze in the air; the rest is nearly black. Ten steps away an impossibly tall, gaunt figure fills the corridor from wall to wall, full body in frame, seen from the side and slightly from the front, bent forward almost double because the ceiling is too low for him: he would stand nearly four metres tall, but his back is arched right under the ceiling tiles like a bridge, the spine a ridge of knobs pressing through pale skin and through the split back seam of a grey hooded sweatshirt far too small for him, its sleeves ending at his elbows. His long thin neck reaches forward and down and his tiny narrow head hangs low, at the height of the locker handles, tilted hard to one side, turned to face the camera. His arms are so long that the knuckles of his huge hands rest on the linoleum, the very long thin fingers spread on the floor like a spider's legs. Two long thin legs bent sharply at the knees, in blue jeans that end at mid-shin and are split at the side seams, bare feet. He was a young man of 18: a real living body with weight, pale grey mottled skin tight over ribs, shoulder blades and knuckles, thin ropy muscle and tendons. The head is small, one twelfth of his length. His face is a small slim mask of cracked old ivory porcelain, narrow and gaunt, hollow cheeks, a pointed chin, two drooping almond eye holes that are pure black with only a pinprick of pale light deep inside each, a thin straight closed mouth slit, a long crack down through the left eye hole, dark tear streaks: the only pale thing in the dark, staring into the lens. Low in the wall beside his head is an open square air vent with its grille hanging off, and he is about to fold himself into it. He is backlit by the far tube: a dark shape with a faint cold edge of light along his back, arms and fingers, his shadow reaching along the floor toward the camera. He is perfectly still. 24mm lens, film grain, deep black shadows, muted cold colour, photographic realism, a real location, real skin and cloth. No blood, no wounds, clean hands.
