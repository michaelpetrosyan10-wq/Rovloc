Dark, underexposed horror film still, real 35mm photograph, night in an American high school corridor: steel lockers along the wall, ceiling tiles, nearly black, one failing fluorescent tube far behind him, cold moonlight from a classroom door window on one side, haze. A low camera three steps from him looks up: a shot from his knees to his head of an impossibly tall, gaunt figure, nearly four metres tall, the tops of the lockers level with his hips, his neck bent to fit under the ceiling. Long evening-shadow proportions, twelve heads tall: a long narrow torso, a long thin neck and, high above, a tiny narrow head. He was a young man of 18. A real living body with weight: pale grey mottled skin tight over ribs, collarbones and hip bones, a sunken stomach, thin ropy muscle and tendons in the bare forearms, narrow shoulders, arms so long that the wrists pass his knees. One huge hand hangs close to the lens, with very long thin fingers and knuckles like knots; that hand is longer and wider than his whole face. The remains of his clothes are far too small: a grey hooded sweatshirt with white drawstrings burst at the seams, its sleeves ending at the elbows, its hem at the lower ribs; blue jeans split at the side seams. He stands upright and too still, the small head cocked to one side, looking down into the lens. His face is a slim mask of cracked old ivory porcelain, only half as wide as it is long, a little too long, slightly lopsided: hollow sunken cheeks under sharp cheekbones, a pointed chin, a sculpted brow ridge with no eyebrows, deep drooping almond eye holes that are pure black with a tiny pale pinprick of light inside each, the right one slightly larger, no glow, a thin nose ridge, a thin closed mouth slit with only its right corner curled slightly up, one long crack from the brow through the left eye hole down to the jaw with hairline cracks off it, a chip missing at the right temple, two dark tear streaks under each eye hole; thin dark hair lies flat on the narrow skull behind it. Expressionless and starved. A thin cold rim of light from behind on his shoulders, arms and fingers; the moonlight grazes half of the mask and the rest is in shadow. 28mm lens, film grain, deep black shadows, muted cold colour, photographic realism, real skin and cloth. No blood, no wounds.
