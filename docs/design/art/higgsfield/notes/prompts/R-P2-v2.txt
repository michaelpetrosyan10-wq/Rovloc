Dark, underexposed horror film still, real 35mm photograph, night in the empty back lot behind an American high school: wet cracked asphalt, a chain-link fence, two dented dark-green steel dumpsters, a steel bike rack with a bicycle lying on its side. One sodium yard lamp on a tall pole burns high up directly behind him, so that it shines from behind his shoulder; nothing lights him from the front. Haze in the air around the lamp. Low camera close to the wet asphalt, twelve steps away, looking up. Beside the dumpsters stands an impossibly tall, gaunt figure, nearly four metres tall, whole from head to bare feet: the dumpsters reach only to his knees. He stands a little stooped, his narrow bony shoulders rounded, his head hanging forward from a neck as long as a forearm and as thick as a wrist, cocked to one side. He is in proportion like a very tall starved man: a long narrow torso, long thin legs, and arms hanging straight down so long that his big old veined hands with long knuckled fingers hang below his knees. A few weeks ago he was a high-school senior of 18. His head is narrow and long and bare, with thin dark hair flat on the skull; his face is not a human face: a very long, very narrow, smooth pale face of old ivory gone leathery, with no nose, tapering down to a point far below where a chin should be, a wrinkled brow, two huge black slanted eye holes with one tiny pinprick of pale light deep in each, two dark streaks running down from them, a small slit of a mouth near the tip: the only pale thing on him, looking straight at the camera. Pale grey veined skin tight over bone; what is left of a grey hooded sweatshirt split along the shoulder and side seams, sleeves stopped at the elbows, hem above his ribs; faded blue jeans ending at mid-shin, burst at the side seams. He is a black silhouette with a thin hard edge of sodium lamplight along his skull, shoulders, arms and fingers; his very long shadow lies across the wet asphalt toward the camera. Perfect stillness. 24mm lens, film grain, deep black shadows, muted colour, photographic realism, a real location, real skin and cloth. Not CG, not a puppet, not a costume, not a skeleton, not a bird. No blood, no wounds, nothing on his head.
