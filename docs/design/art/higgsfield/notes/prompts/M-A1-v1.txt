Horror film still, real 35mm photograph, night, inside a dark American high school corridor, wide shot from a low camera near the floor. In the middle of the corridor stands a boy of 18 whose living body has grown wrong, full body in frame from bare feet to head: nearly four metres tall, twice the height of the steel lockers beside him, and starved thin, his head just under the ceiling tiles. Everything about him is long and narrow, the head too: a small, narrow, gaunt head no bigger than an ordinary man's, high up on a long thin neck, with thin dark hair plastered flat to the skull. His face has turned to cracked old ivory porcelain, like a death mask of a starved young man that covers only the front of the face: long and very narrow, twice as long as it is wide, slightly lopsided, hollow temples, sharp cheekbones over caved-in cheeks, a narrow pointed chin, a sculpted brow ridge without eyebrows, small drooping almond eye holes that are pure black with one tiny pale pinprick of light deep inside each, a thin nose ridge, a thin closed mouth slit, nearly straight, only its right corner slightly curled, one long crack from the brow through the left eye hole down to the jaw, hairline cracks, a chip missing at the right temple, two dark tear streaks under each eye hole. Blank, starved, staring straight at the camera. His body: pale grey mottled living skin tight over ribs, collarbones and hip bones, a hollow stomach, thin ropy muscle and tendons, narrow shoulders, bare forearms, arms so long that the fingertips hang below his knees, very long thin fingers. He wears the remains of a teenager's clothes far too small for him: a grey hoodie burst at the seams, its sleeves ending at his elbows and its hem at his lower ribs, white drawstrings; blue jeans split at the sides that end at mid-shin. He stands upright and perfectly still, arms hanging, head tilted a few degrees. The corridor is almost black: one failing fluorescent tube far behind him and cold moonlight from classroom door windows; he is a backlit shape with a thin cold rim of light along shoulders, arms and fingers; the porcelain face dimly catches the light. Haze in the air, a wet shine on the linoleum. 24mm lens, film grain, deep shadows, muted cold colour, photographic realism, real skin and cloth. No blood, no wounds.
