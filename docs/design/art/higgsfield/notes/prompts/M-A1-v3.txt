Dark, underexposed horror film still, real 35mm photograph. A long American high school corridor at night seen down its length from a low camera: steel lockers on both walls, ceiling tiles, linoleum with a wet shine, one flickering fluorescent tube at the far end, haze in the air; the rest is nearly black. Twenty steps away, in the middle of the corridor, backlit by the far light, stands an impossibly tall, impossibly thin figure, full body in frame from bare feet to head: nearly four metres tall, so tall that the tops of the lockers are level with his hips and he has to bend his long neck to fit under the ceiling tiles. His proportions are those of a long evening shadow: twelve heads tall, with a tiny narrow head at the top of a long thin neck, narrow shoulders, arms so long that the fingertips hang below his knees, very long thin fingers, long thin legs. He is alive, not a statue: living pale grey mottled skin, his weight on one leg, one shoulder lower than the other, the small head cocked to one side. He was a young man of 18 and still wears the remains of his clothes, far too small now: a grey hooded sweatshirt with white drawstrings, burst at the seams, its sleeves ending at his elbows and its hem at his lower ribs; blue jeans that end at mid-shin, split at the side seams; bare feet. Skin tight over ribs and hip bones shows in the gap, a sunken stomach. His face is a small slim mask of cracked old ivory porcelain, narrow and gaunt, hollow cheeks, a pointed chin, two drooping almond eye holes that are pure black with only a pinprick of pale light deep inside each, no glow, a thin straight closed mouth slit, a long crack down through the left eye hole, dark tear streaks. It is the only pale spot on a dark shape and it is turned to the camera. He stands upright and perfectly still. A thin cold rim of light runs along his shoulders, arms and long fingers; his long thin shadow lies along the floor toward the camera. 24mm lens, film grain, deep black shadows, muted cold colour, photographic realism, a real location, real skin and cloth. No blood, no wounds.
