Horror film still, real 35mm photograph, dusk, the empty back lot behind an American high school: wet cracked asphalt, a chain-link fence, two dented green steel dumpsters, a bike rack with one bicycle fallen on its side, a brick wall with steel staff doors, the last deep blue light in the sky. One sodium yard lamp on a tall pole burns behind the figure. Wide shot from a low camera, twelve steps away. In the middle of the lot, between the camera and the lamp, full body in frame, an impossibly tall, gaunt figure is bent far forward, hunched almost double: he would stand nearly four metres tall, but his back is arched high like a bridge, higher than the dumpster lids and the fence, the spine a ridge of knobs under pale skin and under the split back seam of a grey hooded sweatshirt far too small for him, its sleeves ending at his elbows. His long thin neck reaches forward and down and his tiny narrow head hangs low, tilted hard to one side, turned to the camera. His arms are so long that the knuckles of his huge hands rest on the asphalt in front of his bare feet, the very long thin fingers spread on the ground like a spider's legs. Two long thin legs bent at the knees, in blue jeans that end at mid-shin and are split at the side seams. He was a young man of 18: a real living body with weight, pale grey mottled skin tight over ribs, shoulder blades and knuckles, thin ropy muscle and tendons. His proportions are those of a long evening shadow, the head small, one twelfth of his length. His face is a small slim mask of cracked old ivory porcelain, narrow and gaunt, hollow cheeks, a pointed chin, two drooping almond eye holes that are pure black with only a pinprick of pale light deep inside each, no glow, a thin straight closed mouth slit, a long crack down through the left eye hole, dark tear streaks: the only pale spot on a dark shape, staring into the lens. He is backlit by the lamp, almost a silhouette: an orange rim of light along the arch of his spine, his arms and his fingers, his long shadow lying across the wet asphalt toward the camera. On the asphalt in the foreground lie a red knitted beanie and a pair of empty white sneakers with the laces tied. He is perfectly still. 28mm lens, film grain, deep shadows, muted colour, photographic realism, a real location, real skin and cloth. No blood, no wounds.
