Dark, underexposed horror film still, real 35mm photograph, night in an American high school corridor: steel lockers on both walls, ceiling tiles, one flickering fluorescent tube far down the corridor behind him, haze; the rest is nearly black. Close three-quarter shot at eye height, an arm's length from his face. An impossibly tall, gaunt figure, nearly four metres tall, has bent down to the camera: his shoulders and arched back are far above, pressed under the ceiling tiles, in a grey hooded sweatshirt far too small for him and split along the back seam, and his long neck curves forward and down from them like a heron's, as thick as a wrist, living pale grey mottled skin with tendons and the knobs of the vertebrae showing under it, so that his small narrow head hangs level with the lens, tilted hard to one side. One of his huge hands rests on the locker doors beside the head, bare forearm with ropy tendons, very long thin fingers with knuckles like knots; the hand is longer and wider than his whole face. He was a young man of 18; thin dark hair lies flat on the narrow skull. His face is a slim mask of cracked old ivory porcelain, only half as wide as it is long, a little too long, slightly lopsided: hollow sunken cheeks under sharp cheekbones, a pointed chin, a sculpted brow ridge with no eyebrows, deep drooping almond eye holes that are pure black with a tiny pale pinprick of light far inside each, the right one slightly larger, a thin nose ridge, a thin closed mouth slit, almost straight, with only its right corner curled slightly up, one long crack from the brow through the left eye hole down to the jaw with hairline cracks off it, a chip missing at the right temple, two dark tear streaks under each eye hole. Expressionless, starved, looking straight into the lens from very near. He is perfectly still. He is backlit by the far tube: a faint cold edge of light on the neck, the fingers and the rim of the mask, the front of the mask only dimly lit, most of him lost in black. 35mm lens, shallow focus on the mask, film grain, deep black shadows, muted cold colour, photographic realism, real skin, real glazed ceramic. No blood, no wounds, clean hands.
