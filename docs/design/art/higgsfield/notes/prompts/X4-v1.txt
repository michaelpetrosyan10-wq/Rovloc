Dark, underexposed horror film still, real 35mm photograph, nightfall in the empty back lot behind an American high school: wet cracked asphalt, a chain-link fence, two dented green steel dumpsters, a bike rack with a bicycle lying on its side, a black backpack dropped on the ground. One sodium yard lamp on a tall pole burns directly behind the figure; nothing lights him from the front. Low camera close to the asphalt, eight steps away, looking up. Against the lamp glow an impossibly tall, gaunt figure is still unfolding to his full height of nearly four metres, already far taller than the dumpsters and the fence: he stands in profile, his knees and his back not yet straight, his long arms hanging so that the fingertips are below his knees, very long thin fingers; only his head has turned to face the camera, cocked to one side, and the body has not followed it. Long evening-shadow proportions: twelve heads tall, a tiny narrow head on a long thin neck, narrow shoulders, two long thin legs. A minute ago he was a young man of 18: his grey hooded sweatshirt has split along the shoulder and side seams, its sleeves now end at his elbows and its hem at his lower ribs; his blue jeans end at mid-shin, burst at the side seams; bare feet; pale grey skin tight over the ribs. His face is a small slim mask of cracked old ivory porcelain, narrow and gaunt, hollow cheeks, a pointed chin, two drooping almond eye holes that are pure black with only a pinprick of pale light deep inside each, a thin straight closed mouth slit, a long crack down through the left eye hole, dark tear streaks: the only pale thing on him, looking at the camera. He is a black silhouette with a thin hard edge of lamplight along the back of his neck, his spine, his arms and his fingers; the lamp flares in the haze behind his shoulder; his very long shadow lies across the wet asphalt and passes under the camera. In the foreground, sharp on the asphalt: a pair of empty white sneakers side by side with the laces still tied, and a red knitted beanie. Perfect stillness. 24mm lens, film grain, deep black shadows, muted colour, photographic realism, a real location, real skin and cloth. No blood, no wounds, clean hands.
