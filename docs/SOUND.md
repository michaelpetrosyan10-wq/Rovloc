# Sound

How ROVLOC's sounds are made, measured and shipped. The code is `web/sounds/`; the recordings every sound is made
from are in `web/sounds/sources/`; the output is `web/build/audio/` (git-ignored) and the sound board
`web/build/soundboard/`.

```
npm run sounds            render every voice (or: npm run sounds -- --only <family|Name>[,...]   --force   --jobs 1|2)
npm run sounds:audit      measure every render against its class's rules (-- --group reference|world|air, --only, --no-sheets)
npm run sounds:test       the kit's, the recording layer's and the measurements' tests
npm run sounds:format     decode every shipped file in headless Chromium and compare it with its render
npm run sounds:board      build the sound board and check it in headless Chromium (-- --no-check)
npm run sounds:import     copy the recordings the voices name from a downloaded library into sources/ (-- --from <dir> [--prune])
npm run sounds:probe      measure candidate recordings (-- --from <dir> <lib>/<pack>[/<regex>] [--rate r] [--lp Hz] [--events])
```

## 1. Why

The player, in order:

1. *"i want the sounds to actually be scary. Rn its like this vibecoded high pitched sound. I also want his footsteps
   to be louder so people know when hes around ... Make the sounds of the monster be scary and his footsteps be scary
   and other sounds be scary too like the glass its all lame sounds"*
2. After hearing 64 sounds generated by a synthesis kit: *"Its got like this wooden thing like the clicks yk they dont
   sound good fix that reference other horror games use their sounds"*
3. After hearing candidates made from real recordings: *"Pick the scariest low ones for Jumpscare do b or c and change
   glass i dont like it right now then just test it yourself dont gotta send to me again"*

So generated sounds are out as the source: they read as wooden knocks and dry clicks. Every sound of the game is now
made the way other horror games make theirs: from **recordings** (foley, animal and human vocals, ambiences), slowed,
layered and put in a room. We never copy another game's files. The player will not listen again before the beta, so
every choice below is made by measurement and by horror practice, and tested by the machine (section 12).

## 2. The cast

`web/sounds/cast.json` lists every sound the game needs (169): the SynthPresets, every inline recipe in `web/src`
(creature, objectives, parkour, radar, story), the ambience director's `Dread*` presets, and the names of stages
that do not exist yet. Each entry has its sources (where the need comes from), what plays it, an owner group
(reference: him, his steps, your steps, glass, scares; world: what a player or a machine does at a place; air:
atmosphere, weather, distant events, story) and a status:

- `voiced`: made from recordings, rendered and audited (every sound the current game can play: 143 names);
- `waiting`: not played by the current game (its stage or feature does not exist yet: the airport, the city, the
  talking monster's beds, his arrival cue); listed, never required;
- `dropped`: with a note saying why (`CreatureStepDuct`: he never enters a vent).

## 3. The law: recordings first

1. **Every sound is a recipe over recordings** (`lib/recipe.mjs`): layers of (recording, region, playback rate,
   filters, gain, delay). A kit layer may only add what no small recording carries (a sub thump under an impact, a
   room, a soft palate's flutter on a throat); bare synthesis is never the body of a sound. The audit refuses a voice
   without `sources`.
2. **Slower and lower is the horror tool.** A recording is pitched the way tape is (`kit/sample.mjs resample`: played
   at 0.5 it is an octave down and twice as long). Nothing is time-stretched or pitch-shifted on its own.
3. **Weight that survives a laptop speaker.** A heavy sound keeps 30 % of its energy under 250 Hz and loses under 9 dB
   through the laptop filter (12 dB/octave high-pass at 220 Hz): a slowed body under a mid layer that a small speaker
   plays, the whole driven into a soft curve (`densify`) so the sub's harmonics reach the speaker.
4. **No dry clicks, no shrill treble.** His sounds hold at most 5 % of their energy over 2.5 kHz; a heavy impact at
   most 3 % of its first 12 ms over 1.5 kHz (the "click"); everything but glass, sparks and keys has its centroid
   under 3 kHz.
5. **Never the same twice.** What repeats has 3-6 variants from **different takes**, at least 2 dB apart.
6. **Quiet is part of it, never painful.** Beds integrate at most -34 LUFS (they sit at -34 to -38); the jumpscare is
   the loudest thing (-13.3 LUFS), and at the output at most 24 dB over the Basement's beds, so a player who turns
   the quiet up is not hurt by it (the stingers sit under it, -14.5 to -16.5); nothing but a scare is louder than his
   footsteps (-14 to -15.4); all of it peak-limited at -1 dBTP.
7. **What sounds like him is him** (spec I1). His signatures are the THROAT (the 18-38 Hz pulsing of a slowed rasp,
   measured as the rattle index, at least 0.2 in every variant) and the GAIT (heavy-class weight). A recording used
   by any of his sounds is used by no sound that is not his (the audit checks every id), and no breath or vocal that
   is not his carries the rattle.
8. **No gore** (spec I12): the catch and the trap are dull, dry and abrupt (a body blow, a muffled crack through
   cloth), never wet.

## 4. The audit

`audit.mjs` measures every render's raw samples (`web/build/audio/raw/`) with `lib/measure.mjs`, holds it to the
universal rules, the one-shot or loop rules, its class's rules and the defaults (`lib/classes.mjs`), and fails on any
breach; on a cast sound that is not `waiting` or `dropped` without a render; on a voice missing from the cast; on a
recording not in `sources/SOURCES.json`, not CC0, or not stored with its recorded SHA-256; on one of his recordings in
a sound that is not his; on shipped audio over the budget; on the distance rule; and on the heaviest rule.

**Every sound:** no clipped samples; true peak at most -0.9 dBTP; |mean| at most 0.0025; first and last sample at
least 40 dB under the peak; loudness within 1.5 LU of its declared reference; centroid at most 3 kHz unless its class
is bright; purity at most 0.45 unless its class is a resonance (then at least 5 peaks); variants at least 2 dB apart.
**One-shots:** under 12 ms of silence before; the last 5 ms at least 50 dB down; at most 2.5 s (stingers 6 s; baked
sounds 5 s). **Loops:** 8-24 s; the seam's sample jump at most 1.5 x the 99.9th percentile of the steps inside; no
level step across the seam beyond the loop's own. **His sounds:** at least one declared signature in every variant;
at most 5 % over 2.5 kHz. **Not his:** a breath or vocal has a rattle index under 0.2.

| Class | Rules | What its picture looks like |
|---|---|---|
| heavy | low (< 250 Hz) >= 30 %; laptop loss < 9 dB; attack <= 5 ms; centroid <= 1.5 kHz; click <= 3 % | a vertical wall at the onset whose low end smears and decays; most of it under 600 Hz |
| body | low >= 15 %; laptop loss < 12 dB; attack <= 5 ms | a short broadband onset over a low-mid body that decays fast |
| light | attack <= 5 ms; <= 0.9 s; loudness <= -24 LUFS; centroid <= 2.5 kHz | a small, short, dull smudge low in the picture |
| glass (bright, resonance) | high (> 2.5 kHz) >= 20 %; peaks >= 5; attack <= 5 ms | a spray of short high lines over a low thump |
| crunch (bright) | purity <= 0.2; attack <= 5 ms; <= 1.2 s | a dense cloud of tiny grains, no lines |
| keys (bright, resonance) | attack <= 5 ms; <= 0.6 s; peaks >= 5 | a handful of short high lines |
| spark (bright) | attack <= 5 ms; click <= 40 %; high <= 50 % | scattered vertical ticks over a broken buzz (a crackle over a hum, never a train of dry clicks) |
| bell (bright, resonance) | peaks >= 5; attack <= 5 ms | a few strokes, each a stack of long inharmonic lines |
| liquid | purity <= 0.3; peaks >= 5 | a cloud of bubbles, denser where it splashes |
| breath | rise >= 40 ms; purity <= 0.25; centroid <= 2.5 kHz | a moving cloud with formant bands; no hard edges |
| vocal | roughness >= 0.12; centroid <= 2.5 kHz | formant bands smeared by roughness |
| friction | centroid <= 2.5 kHz | fine vertical striations over a body |
| swell | rise >= 80 ms; centroid <= 2.5 kHz | a cloud that grows (or is drawn in) smoothly |
| stinger (<= 6 s) | low >= 15 %; laptop loss < 9 dB; roughness >= 0.1; loudness <= -8 LUFS; attack <= 20 ms | a sudden dense wall with a low drop |
| music (loop) | low >= 20 %; laptop loss < 9 dB; loudness <= -14 LUFS | regular vertical hits over a low band; no seam |
| bed (loop) | loudness <= -34 LUFS; centroid <= 1.5 kHz | a low even cloud; nothing stands out; no seam |
| far (<= 5 s) | centroid <= 1.5 kHz | a dark, smeared event in a long tail |
| menu (<= 0.3 s) | centroid <= 2 kHz; loudness <= -26 LUFS | one tiny low dot |

Loudness is ITU BS.1770 (a one-shot's loudest 400 ms block, a loop's gated integrated loudness). "Laptop loss" is
that loudness minus the same through the laptop filter. "Attack" is the 10-90 % rise of the first local maximum of
the 1 ms envelope that reaches 20 % of its peak. The rattle index is the 18-38 Hz modulation depth of the
100-1,200 Hz band's envelope; the click the share of the first 12 ms' energy over 1.5 kHz.

**The distance rule (his footsteps):** through the game's own roll-off (`presets.js`, from `CreatureConfig.Footfalls`
and each preset's roll-off; `web/src/engine/audio/rolloff.js`), his quietest take is at least 10 dB over the loudest
take of a player's footstep on the matching surface at 10, 40 and `Footfalls.HeardTo` studs, and at `HeardTo` it
stands at least 6 dB over the Basement's dread bed (the player: "louder so people know when hes around"). **The heaviest rule:** no sound that is not his, a scare or music has a
reference loudness within 1 LU of his footsteps'.

**Pictures:** `web/build/audio/audit/<family>-<n>.png`, eight sounds a sheet: a log-frequency spectrogram over the
waveform with the key numbers (a name in red failed a rule).

## 5. Recordings

`web/sounds/sources/<lib>/<pack>/<file>` holds every recording a voice uses, **unmodified** (a WAV over 1 MB is
stored as FLAC, the same samples bit for bit: the import compares the decoded PCM's MD5 before keeping it, and
records the WAV's name, size and SHA-256 as `original`). `web/sounds/sources/SOURCES.json` records each pack's page,
its author as the page's "Author:" field names them (`submitter`: who uploaded it, when the page names someone else:
"Independent.nu (Submitted by qubodup)"), `links`: the links its description gives (the author's site or game, or a
work it was derived from), its licences and archive, and each file's SHA-256, size and path inside the pack. The
import reads the author, the submitter and the links from the page itself (the library keeps each opengameart.org
page as `page.html` beside its `source.json`) and re-reads every pack's record on each run: nothing in SOURCES.json is
written by hand. `lib = kenney` is kenney.nl
(impact-sounds, rpg-audio, interface-sounds); `lib = oga` is opengameart.org. **Only packs whose page states CC0 are
used**; a recording that is not in the index, whose pack is not CC0, or whose bytes differ from the recorded hash
cannot be rendered (`lib/sources.mjs`), and the audit and `sounds:test` check the whole store.

No file is taken from a game's assets, and no pack whose page says its sounds come from a game, from an unnamed
source or from a commercial sample library is used (three such packs were removed: nhfea-sound, "from the game Never
Heard From Ever Again"; yd's platformer sounds, taken with a game's sprites from sources its author cannot name;
horror-hit-soundpack-1, made from BandLab's drum-machine samples). A few packs were recorded by their own authors for
games of their own and released by them as CC0: different-steps (for Minetest), mutant-frog and scrapes (Stendhal),
swishes (Summoning Wars), big-scary-troll-sounds (Mortila, a Hexen mod), the electricity pack (faxcorp's Short
Circuit). No recording is derived from a work whose licence cannot be read here: the four whose pages name a Freesound
or Wikimedia Commons original (electric-buzz, large-wings-flap, tree-creaking, doorbell-ring) were replaced. Two packs
say they were made from other recordings without a link: different-steps ("recordings from pdsounds.org", public
domain, in its author's comment) and small-dino-raspy-calls ("synthesized from some CC0 horse snorts").

The store was filled by `npm run sounds:import -- --from <library>` from a library of downloaded packs (each with
the `source.json` its fetch wrote from the pack's page); `--prune` drops what no voice names. After that the repo
needs no library: `npm run sounds` decodes `sources/` with ffmpeg once into `web/build/audio/cache` (keyed by the
file's hash) and renders from there.

## 6. The kit (`web/sounds/kit/`): the processing toolbox

Plain JavaScript DSP over Float32Arrays, run in Node, deterministic (every random number comes from `rng.mjs`, seeded
from the sound's name and variant and forked per layer; `Math.random` is banned and a test checks).

| Module | What a recipe uses it for |
|---|---|
| sample | `resample` (tape-speed pitch: a band-limited read at any rate, constant or bending), `region`, `active`, `events` (the takes of a long recording) |
| filters | RBJ biquads, Butterworth cascades, the laptop filter |
| mix | gains, fades, offsets, wrapped loops, crossfades |
| impact | `unit`: every layer brought to unit peak before its gain |
| thump | the pitch-dropping saturated sub under an impact (`withSub`) |
| shape, dynamics | 4x oversampled saturation (a layer's `drive`); `densify`; the true-peak limiter |
| voice | `flutter`: the soft palate's 20-35 Hz pulsing laid on his throat recordings (a layer's `flutter`) |
| reverb | the game's spaces as impulse responses (`inSpace`, `inSpaceMono`: a sound baked into a room) |
| loudness, fft | BS.1770 loudness, true peak; FFT, STFT, convolution |
| delay, env, rng | the reverb's delay lines; segments and decay factors; the seeded random numbers |
| distance | the board's distance model (Near / Mid / Far) |
| noise, the state-variable filter, the slow modulators of env | the tests' signal sources (noise, swept and decaying test tones) |

The synthesis that made the rejected first set (modal bodies, friction, grains, Karplus-Strong, the formant voices,
the footfall builder) is gone: nothing is made from it.

## 7. Adding a sound

1. Find it in `cast.json`. Pick its family file `web/sounds/voices/<family>.mjs` (one manifest per family).
2. Find recordings: `npm run sounds:probe -- --from <library> oga/<pack>[/<regex>] --rate 0.6 --lp 2400` prints each
   candidate's loudness, laptop loss, low share, centroid, attack, roughness, pitch, click and rattle as a recipe would
   play it (`--events` lists the takes of a long file). Only CC0 packs.
3. Write the voice with `recorded({...})` (`lib/recipe.mjs` documents every layer field: `src`, `from`/`to`, `rate`,
   `bend`, `reverse`, `trim`, `hp`/`lp`, `eq`, `drive`, `flutter`, `fadeIn`/`fadeOut`, `env`, `gain`, `at`, `pan`,
   `chance`) and an optional `finish` (`withSub`, `inSpace`, `inSpaceMono`, `ending`, kit filters). `lib/voices.mjs`
   documents the voice fields (`class`, `him`, `repeats`, `variants`, `channels`, `loop`, `space`, `loudness`,
   `first`, `board`). No distance goes in a voice: how far a sound carries is its preset's roll-off.
4. `npm run sounds:import -- --from <library>` (copies the new recordings and records them), `npm run sounds -- --only
   <family>`, `npm run sounds:audit -- --only <family>`; look at the sheets; set the cast status to `voiced`.
5. A new name the game plays gets its preset metadata in `src/ReplicatedStorage/Shared/Audio/SynthPresets.luau` and
   `npm run presets` regenerates `web/src/engine/audio/presets.js` (or it is an inline recipe in `web/src`, which
   keeps its own bus, roll-off and caption).
6. Never loosen a rule to pass; a rule wrong for a whole class is fixed for the class, with the reason here.

The pipeline (`lib/pipeline.mjs`) finishes every render: an 18 Hz high-pass; a one-shot's leading silence and dead
tail trimmed with a 20 ms fade; a 0.3 ms fade-in; scaled to its reference loudness; limited to -1 dBTP (a loop over
three periods, the middle one kept, shipped with 100 ms of its own signal either side of its loop points). A
content-hash cache (the kit, the pipeline, the recipe and recording layer, SOURCES.json, the family file, the name
and variant) skips what has not changed; at most two worker threads, under `nice -n 10`.

## 8. The shipped format: MP3

MP3 (LAME VBR `-q:a 4`, 48 kHz, mono unless the sound is 2D), because every browser's `decodeAudioData` decodes it
and Roblox accepts it. Every flag that could make the bytes depend on the machine is pinned, so the same render gives
the same file. `npm run sounds:format` decodes every shipped file in headless Chromium and compares it with its
render (offset, length, third-octave spectrum, transients, loop seams).

## 9. Budgets

All shipped audio at most 8 MB (the audit checks; today 5.3 MB in 412 files: the first pack 2.36 MB, 72 sounds, the
rest 2.78 MB, 71); one-shots at most 2.5 s (stingers 6 s, baked far events 5 s); loops 8-24 s; mono unless the sound
is heard all around you (beds, rain, wind, the chase music, 2D stingers). The first pack is downloaded and decoded
before the first frame (about 35 MB of samples): it is part of what a Chromebook on Potato waits for. Decoded samples
at most 60 MB at any time on a 4 GB Chromebook (section 11). The lite build carries every sound as base64 (about a
third more than the MP3s). The recordings in `sources/` (408 files, 43 MB, committed to git) are kept whole and
unmodified (their licence record is per file); they are not shipped.

## 10. The sound board

`npm run sounds:board` builds `web/build/soundboard/` (a static page plus the audio). A big play button per sound
cycles its variants; Near / Mid / Far (10 / 40 / 80 studs) play a positional sound through its preset's roll-off and
the corridor's impulse response. It checks itself in headless Chromium.

## 11. How the game plays them

- **The bank** (`web/src/engine/audio/bank.js`). The build (`web/tools/assets.mjs soundPacks`) packs every rendered
  MP3 end to end into two packs, `first` (what must never be late: him, his and your footsteps, the stingers and the
  chase, the warnings (TorchStutter before a teleport, TeleportHiss, HideWarn, HideKick), your body, the menu clicks
  of the title screen) and `rest` (the beds, which fade in over seconds anyway, doors, lockers, machines and everything
  else), with an index in the page's manifest (`audio.sounds`: name -> pack, channels, decode rate,
  loop points, variants as [offset, bytes, seconds]). A site ships the packs as hashed assets; a single-file page
  (the lite build, every test page) carries them as base64. The build stops with a clear message when a render is
  missing or stale (`npm run sounds` first).
- **Decoding.** Before the first frame the game decodes the `first` set (awaited in the engine's warm-up); the rest
  is decoded in the background one sound at a time, while it fits; a sound asked for before its turn is decoded at
  once (a one-shot plays if it is ready within 0.3 s). Decoded samples stay under 60 MB: past it the sounds played
  longest ago are dropped (never a playing or a first-set one) and decoded again when next needed. A dark sound (at
  most a small share of its energy over 9 kHz) is decoded at 24 kHz, half the memory. `decodeAudioData` runs off the
  main thread: nothing on the frame path.
- **Playing** (`audio.js play()`). A name the bank has plays one of its files (never the same variant twice in a row;
  `opts.seed` picks the same one on every co-op client) through the same bus, panner, roll-off, priority, voice
  limits, ducking, caption and room handling as before; a looped voice loops the file between its loop points, or,
  for a pulse preset (`repeat`: a heartbeat, a breath, a drip), plays another variant every interval. A name without
  a file keeps its old recipe, so nothing is ever silent. `Audio.recipeNames` lists the names on the old path (today:
  none, see the audio test).
- **The output ceiling.** After the master's safety compressor (whose automatic makeup gain lifted the jumpscare to
  -0.1 dBFS at the output in the audio test) a waveshaper holds every sample under -1.1 dBFS: unity under 0.6, a tanh
  shoulder above it. Nothing quieter than -4.4 dBFS is touched.
- **His footsteps** are his own sounds: `creature/view.js _onFootstep` plays `CreatureStep{Walk,Chase}<surface>`
  (SynthPresets `CreatureStepSurface` maps the eight surfaces onto his five), heard as far as
  `CreatureConfig.Footfalls` says (roll-off 24..160 studs, heard to 80); every distance is in the shared config,
  none in a file. Out of sight within earshot his rig steps every third frame with the time of all three
  (`view.js LodClock`), so his footfalls keep their pace at any frame rate (the audio test walks him at 10, 20 and
  30 fps).
- **The Roblox port** has the committed index of the renders, `src/ReplicatedStorage/Shared/Audio/RecordedSounds.luau`
  (written by `npm run sounds`, compared with the manifest by `web/tools/data-test.mjs`): every name's files, their
  seconds, a loop's region and its channels. The port uploads the MP3s, plays a looped file's region
  (`Sound.LoopRegion`, never the whole file: the file carries 100 ms of its own signal either side) and plays each
  name through the same preset metadata (SynthPresets: bus, roll-off, priority, voice limits, a pulse's repeat).
- **The lite build** embeds every sound (the shipped MP3s, base64) so it plays the same files as the site; the audio
  test checks the decoded budget on it.

## 12. The choices, measured

The tools: the audit's measurements on the rendered files; `web/tools/audio-test.mjs` in the real game (headless
Chromium, on the game test page that `tools/game-build.mjs` builds, the sound packs inline as in the lite build): every
shipped file decodes; every name the game can play resolves to a file (none on the old path); every variant of his
footstep against every variant of a player's through the game's own engine at 10, 30 and `HeardTo` studs; every
variant of his step at `HeardTo` against the Basement's drone and dread bed as the game plays them; his footfalls per
stud walked out of sight at 10, 20 and 30 fps; the jumpscare the loudest file, under -1 dBFS and at most 24 dB over
the beds at the output; the decoded-PCM budget on every stage; a run with the sound on and a clean console. Numbers
below are ranges over the variants.

- **His footsteps** (the lead's recipe B, "a dull body thud", the lowest of the four candidates): centroid 70-131 Hz,
  88-98 % of the energy under 250 Hz, laptop loss 5.6-8.4 dB, click 0-1.6 %, -14 to -15.4 LUFS. Through the game's own
  engine (the audio test) his quietest variant is at least 18.9 dB over a player's loudest at 10, 30 and 80 studs
  (at 80 a player's walking step is past its roll-off: silent); at 80 studs (as far as `CreatureConfig.Footfalls` says
  he is heard; roll-off 28..160) his variants arrive at -28.0 to -30.4 LUFS over the Basement's drone and dread bed at
  -37.7: 7.3 to 9.7 dB over them (the rule: at least 6). With the package's first roll-off (14..160) the margin was
  1.4-3.6 dB.
- **His growl**: deep snarls at 0.6-0.66 under mid snarls at 0.62-0.68 and the deep roar at 0.74-0.8: centroid
  177-338 Hz, 54-84 % under 250 Hz, roughness 0.4-0.5, rattle 0.2-0.3, laptop loss 0.9-3.4 dB (it reads on a small
  speaker), at most 1 % over 2.5 kHz.
- **His breath and sniff**: the troll's idle breaths at 0.66-0.72 with the throat's flutter, in a duct (centroid
  about 200-700 Hz, rattle 0.31-0.36); slowed nose and breath takes for the sniff.
- **His rattle**: slowed burbles, a mutant frog and a raspy call at 0.44-0.5 over a slowed snore: wet and organic,
  rattle 0.35, nothing over 2.5 kHz.
- **The jumpscare: recipe B** (a snarl attack at 0.8-0.86 driven 8 dB, a roar at 0.66-0.72, an attack, the deep roar,
  a sub drop 62 -> 34 Hz) over C (a slowed creature scream, a roar and an attack, the same finish), chosen at the same
